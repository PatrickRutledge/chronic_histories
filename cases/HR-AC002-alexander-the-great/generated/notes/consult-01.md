## Recommendations

1. Lung volumes and gas transfer today confirm mild extraparenchymal restriction with normal DLCO; no further tests are needed now (question 1).
2. CT shows no retained fragment, fistula or loculated fluid; no further imaging unless symptoms change (question 2).
3. No surgery: decortication is kept for a trapped lung or severe restriction, and a localized fibrothorax with a TLC of 79 percent is neither (question 3).
4. Lidocaine 5% patch over the scar for up to 12 hours a day, with acetaminophen within 2,000 mg a day; avoid opioids, tramadol, gabapentinoids and benzodiazepines while he drinks heavily; a telephone check in four weeks; an intercostal nerve block if pain stays above 4 of 10 after six weeks (question 4).
5. Physical therapy for thoracic mobility and scar desensitization, deep-breathing exercises and graded aerobic and strength training back to long marches; he may continue full duty (question 5).
6. Repeat spirometry in about two months; then yearly, or sooner if breathlessness returns (question 5).
7. Return sooner for fever, cough with sputum or blood, new pleuritic pain or worsening breathlessness.
