Teaching objective: Recognize a persistent unilateral throat lesion with painful swallowing in a heavy tobacco user as possible head and neck cancer, refer urgently for laryngoscopy and biopsy with timely imaging, and provide primary-care supportive care around diagnosis: tobacco cessation, nutrition, dental preparation, symptom control, mood screening, and laboratory monitoring.
