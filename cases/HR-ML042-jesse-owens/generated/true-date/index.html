Teaching objective: Find lung cancer early in a 65-year-old Black man who has smoked a pack a day for 35 years and who was never offered screening: confirm eligibility on the USPSTF 1974 criteria (50 to 80 years, 20 pack-years or more, smoking now or quit within 15 years), hold the shared decision-making visit with a decision aid and tobacco treatment that the CMS coverage decision requires, read the low-dose CT with Lung-RADS, and work up a category 4X nodule without delay: PET-CT, brain MRI, systematic EBUS staging of the mediastinum (ACCP), and tissue for PD-L1 and comprehensive biomarker testing before any immunotherapy. Then treat resectable stage IIB adenocarcinoma on the NCCN guideline: tumor board review, physiologic evaluation for lobectomy (ACCP), three cycles of nivolumab with carboplatin and pemetrexed before surgery (CheckMate 816), immune-related adverse event monitoring, thoracoscopic lobectomy with lymph node dissection, and surveillance CT; treat tobacco dependence with varenicline even after the diagnosis; and track each step for delay, because Black Americans are less often found eligible for and offered screening and less often offered surgery for early lung cancer.
