Teaching objective: Recognize ovarian cancer early in a woman of 39 with persistent bloating, early fullness, and pelvic pain, the symptoms that went unexplained for ten months in Gilda Radner's life: apply the ovarian cancer symptom index (Goff), examine the abdomen and pelvis, and order a CA-125 and a transvaginal ultrasound at the first visit; CT staging and an image-guided biopsy within the week; prompt gynecologic oncology care with primary cytoreductive surgery aiming for no visible residual disease; carboplatin and paclitaxel with antiemetic, neuropathy and blood-count monitoring and extended thromboprophylaxis; germline genetic counseling and testing for every woman with epithelial ovarian cancer, with tumor testing for BRCA1 and BRCA2; PARP inhibitor maintenance chosen from the tumor result; and survivorship care with distress screening and peer support, on the NCCN ovarian cancer guideline, the ASCO germline and somatic testing and PARP inhibitor guidelines, and ACOG Committee Opinion 716.
