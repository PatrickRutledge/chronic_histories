Disposition
Outpatient care with close follow-up. She has no thoughts of suicide or self-harm now and had none during the admission. Her risk is higher in the months after a first episode, and especially in a depressive phase after mania, so a written safety plan was made with her, with her own early warning signs (sleeping less, training for hours, racing thoughts, or feeling hopeless) and what to do about each.
