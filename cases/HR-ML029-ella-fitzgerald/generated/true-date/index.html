Teaching objective: Limb-, sight- and heart-protecting primary care for a 70-year-old touring singer with type 2 diabetes, peripheral artery disease with claudication, diabetic macular edema, and coronary bypass surgery the year before: the comprehensive diabetic foot examination (inspection, 10-g monofilament, vibration, pulses) with the ankle- and toe-brachial indices, IWGDF risk grading and a high-risk foot program with podiatry and stage footwear; prompt vascular surgery evaluation, supervised exercise therapy, and a clear plan for revascularization if symptoms limit her life; a high-intensity statin with ezetimibe to an LDL below 55 mg/dL, aspirin with low-dose rivaroxaban, and glucose-lowering medicines with cardiovascular and kidney benefit (empagliflozin, then semaglutide) in place of a sulfonylurea; retinal care with anti-VEGF injections; kidney monitoring; hypoglycemia safety on tour (insulin timing across time zones, a continuous glucose monitor, glucagon, and a show-day plan); and the equity and access steps that make this care reach her, on the ADA Standards of Care 1987, the IWGDF 1984 guidelines, the 1985 ACC/AHA lower extremity PAD guideline, and the CMID diabetes monograph.
