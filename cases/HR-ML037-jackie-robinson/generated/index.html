Teaching objective: Sight-, heart-, limb- and kidney-protecting care for a 52-year-old Black man with long-standing type 2 diabetes who comes in with blurred vision six weeks after the death of his son. The episode covers urgent retina care for proliferative diabetic retinopathy with macular edema and a vitreous hemorrhage (dilated examination, OCT, intravitreal anti-VEGF injections and panretinal photocoagulation, as the AAO Preferred Practice Pattern describes); lowering glucose steadily rather than quickly, with the early worsening of retinopathy after rapid A1c falls and after semaglutide explained and a GLP-1 receptor agonist started only after the retina is stable; an SGLT2 inhibitor for heart and kidney protection; a high-intensity statin with ezetimibe; blood pressure and kidney care; coronary disease found through exertional chest tightness; the ankle-brachial index, foot examination and wound care for a neuropathic heel blister; low-vision services and diabetes tools he can use with poor sight; depression screening and grief support; and the equity of care for a Black man with diabetes complications, under the ADA Standards of Care 2026, the AAO Diabetic Retinopathy Preferred Practice Pattern 2025, the 2024 ACC/AHA PAD and 2023 chronic coronary disease guidelines, and the CMID monographs.
