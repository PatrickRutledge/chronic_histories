## Assessment and plan

1. **Acute viral gastroenteritis, likely norovirus, with mild dehydration** (A08.4). Acute watery, non-bloody diarrhea after a common-source exposure, already improving, with low-grade temperature only. Mild volume depletion: orthostatic change, dry mouth, and a creatinine of 1.21 mg/dL (eGFR 47), up from 0.88 in August, a prerenal rise while taking an angiotensin receptor blocker. Stool testing and antibiotics are not indicated for uncomplicated watery diarrhea of less than a week. **S**
   - Oral rehydration solution in small, frequent amounts, aiming for pale urine; then a normal diet as tolerated.
   - Loperamide may be used for comfort if there is no fever above 38 °C and no blood in the stool.
   - Hand washing with soap and water, which works better than alcohol gel against norovirus; clean bathroom surfaces with a bleach solution; do not prepare food for others until 48 hours after symptoms end.
   - Rise slowly, sit to wash, and have someone nearby when she showers until the light-headedness is gone.
   - Return or call the same day for blood in the stool, temperature above 38.5 °C, no urine for 12 hours, inability to keep fluids down, fainting, or a fall.
2. **Chronic post-traumatic headache on candesartan** (G44.329). Headache with dehydration on two days, managed within her limit of acute medicine on no more than 2 days a week. Candesartan with volume depletion raises the risk of kidney injury. **S**
   - Hold candesartan until she has been eating and drinking normally for a full day, then restart 16 mg daily.
   - Acetaminophen as needed, no more than 3 g a day and within her 2-days-a-week limit; no ibuprofen or other NSAIDs, which can harm the kidneys during dehydration and risk a return of medication-overuse headache.
   - Repeat the basic metabolic panel one to two weeks after restarting candesartan, at the wellness visit.
3. **Provisional narcolepsy without cataplexy on modafinil** (G47.429). More unplanned sleep episodes during an acute illness, as expected with poor sleep and dehydration. **S**
   - Continue modafinil 100 mg each morning once she is keeping fluids down.
   - Keep the two scheduled naps and add rest as needed; sit down for tasks while she is unwell.
