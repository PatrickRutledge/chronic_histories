Procedures and immunizations

Procedures

  • S Routine EEG: normal background with intermittent left frontal slowing; no epileptiform discharges
  • S MRI of the brain: old depressed left frontal skull defect with small underlying encephalomalacia; normal hippocampi; no tumor or other lesion

  • S Overnight polysomnography: apnea-hypopnea index 4 per hour; no periodic limb movements; REM sleep within 15 minutes of sleep onset
  • S Multiple sleep latency test: mean sleep latency 4.5 minutes with two sleep-onset REM periods in five naps
