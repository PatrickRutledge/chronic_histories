Teaching objective: Care for an older man with moderate Alzheimer's disease after a hip fracture: prevent delirium and further falls by removing high-risk drugs, restore function with home therapy, prevent the next fracture with vitamin D and zoledronic acid, stage the dementia and review its treatment, and support the family caregiver with a burden assessment, respite, and advance care planning, on the CMID Alzheimer's disease and hip fracture schedules.

Social, functional, and mental status

Social history

  • S No tobacco use; occasional glass of wine with dinner, stopped after the fracture
  • S Lives at home with his spouse, the primary caregiver, with a home health aide added after the fracture; retired public official
  • S Caregiver burden: spouse's Zarit Burden Interview score 41 of 88 at the first visit (moderate to severe); she reports broken sleep and has stopped seeing friends
  • S Supports: spouse, adult children, a long-time aide, and church friends who visit

Functional status

  • S FAST stage 5 (moderate Alzheimer's disease): needs help choosing clothes and with all instrumental activities of daily living (Lawton IADL 1 of 8); before the fracture he walked independently
  • S At the first visit: walks short distances with a front-wheeled walker and one-person standby; Timed Up and Go 24 seconds; no orthostatic drop (128/70 seated, 124/70 standing)
  • S Neuropsychiatric screen (NPI-Q): evening restlessness and repeated questions, mild; no hallucinations or aggression; Confusion Assessment Method negative for delirium

Mental status

  • S Alert, pleasant, and sociable; tells familiar stories; oriented to person, not to date; MoCA 11 of 30
  • S Mood good by his report and his spouse's; GDS-15 below the threshold for depression

Allergies and medications

Allergies

  • S No known allergy

Medications

  • S Donepezil 10 mg oral tablet, one tablet by mouth daily in the morning
  • S Acetaminophen 500 mg oral tablet, two tablets (1,000 mg) by mouth three times daily
  • S Oxycodone 5 mg oral tablet, one tablet by mouth every six hours as needed for severe pain
  • S Diphenhydramine 25 mg oral capsule, one capsule by mouth at bedtime
  • S Enoxaparin 40 mg/0.4 mL prefilled syringe, inject 40 mg under the skin daily through day 35 after surgery
  • S Cholecalciferol 50 mcg (2,000 units) oral tablet, one tablet by mouth daily
  • S Zoledronic acid 5 mg/100 mL intravenous infusion over at least 15 minutes, once yearly
