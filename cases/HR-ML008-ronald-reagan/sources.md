## Editorial resolutions

- **Recent-death rule.** Reagan died in 2004. The case uses only what Reagan, his physicians, and his presidential library made public, plus contemporary news reports of those disclosures. His stage of dementia, medicines, bone density, and the caregiver burden scores are synthetic. The recent-death review rule applies.
- **Index age.** The modern episode presents Reagan at 90, his age one month after the January 2001 hip fracture. The synthetic date of birth is February 6, 1936 (shift 25 years). The repair is recorded in the modern frame on January 13, 2026, which maps to the documented operation on January 13, 2001; the index visit on February 10, 2026 maps to February 10, 2001, and the last visit on July 14, 2026 maps to July 14, 2001. Past medical history and family history include only events before the index date.
- **Hip fracture coding.** The fracture followed a fall at home in a man of 89 and is coded as an osteoporotic fracture (M80.051D), following the ICD-10-CM rule that a fracture from minor trauma in a patient with osteoporosis takes an M80 code. The fracture pattern (intertrochanteric) and the sliding hip screw are synthetic choices consistent with the reported pin, plate, and screws.
- **Caregiver.** His letter says the family often bears a heavy burden; that statement is the only historical anchor for the caregiver content. The spouse in the episode is unnamed, and her Zarit scores, sleep, and respite are synthetic. The Zarit Burden Interview, FAST stage, Lawton IADL, Timed Up and Go, and NPI-Q have no verified LOINC code in the terminology server and are recorded as narrative; MoCA, the Barthel Index, and GDS-15 are coded.
- **Depression screening.** GDS-15 is used because CMID lists it for dementia; all scores are below 5, so no safety assessment is required. The dementia code F02.B0 does not trigger the safety-assessment rule.
- **Mother’s cause of death.** The Reagan Library states that Nelle Reagan died of complications of Alzheimer’s disease. Another 1962 account is reported to give a different cause; it was not reviewed, so the claim is graded **D** with certainty probable.
- **Not used.** Pneumonia as the immediate cause of death was reported by news outlets but was not found in a fetched source and is not recorded. Speculation that the 1989 head injury or his time in office related to his dementia is not recorded. The 1981 gunshot wound and the cancer operations are listed only as history.
