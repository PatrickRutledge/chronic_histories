History of Present Illness

Admitted four months ago with right-hand weakness; MRI showed a single ring-enhancing left frontoparietal lesion, and stereotactic biopsy showed Toxoplasma. HIV was diagnosed during that admission, with a viral load of 186,000 copies/mL and a CD4 count of 58. The genotype showed no resistance mutations; hepatitis B surface antigen, surface antibody, and core antibody were negative; hepatitis C antibody negative; hepatitis A antibody positive; interferon-gamma release assay negative; Toxoplasma IgG positive. He completed six weeks of pyrimethamine, sulfadiazine, and leucovorin, and has taken trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole double strength twice daily since. Bictegravir, emtricitabine, and tenofovir alafenamide were started two weeks into toxoplasmosis treatment. His right hand has recovered. Today, after about 15 weeks of therapy: viral load 42 copies/mL, CD4 156, hemoglobin 13.0 g/dL, potassium 4.9 mmol/L, creatinine 1.18 mg/dL (0.98 before treatment), ALT 30, LDL 88 mg/dL. Coronary disease: myocardial infarction at 36, quadruple bypass the same year, and a double bypass for recurrent angina at 39, with a blood transfusion during that operation; no angina now. He takes aspirin 81 mg and atorvastatin 80 mg, ezetimibe was added today, and he has sublingual nitroglycerin. PCV20, a first hepatitis B vaccine dose, and a first meningococcal ACWY dose were given today. He has asked that his diagnosis and results be shared with no one without his consent [S]
