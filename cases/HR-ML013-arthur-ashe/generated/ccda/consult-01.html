Assessment

  • HIV infection on antiretroviral therapy (B20). Responding well: the viral load has fallen from 186,000 to 42 copies/mL in about 15 weeks and should become undetectable within the next few months, and the CD4 count has risen from 58 to 156. Bictegravir with emtricitabine and tenofovir alafenamide is a recommended first regimen, has a high barrier to resistance, and has no meaningful interaction with aspirin, atorvastatin, ezetimibe, or nitroglycerin. A regimen boosted with ritonavir or cobicistat would raise atorvastatin levels and limit its dose, efavirenz would lower them, and abacavir has been associated with myocardial infarction and should be avoided in a man with coronary disease. [S]
  • Cerebral toxoplasmosis on maintenance therapy (B58.2). Clinically resolved. Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole double strength twice daily is an accepted maintenance regimen and also prevents Pneumocystis pneumonia. It can stop once the CD4 count has been above 200 for more than six months on ART with a suppressed viral load, provided he remains free of symptoms. Prophylaxis for Mycobacterium avium complex is not needed, because he started fully active ART promptly. [S]
  • Creatinine. The rise from 0.98 to about 1.17 mg/dL is expected and stable. Both bictegravir and trimethoprim block tubular secretion of creatinine without lowering the true filtration rate. Potassium is high-normal on trimethoprim and should be watched. [S]
  • Vaccines. Inactivated vaccines can be given at any CD4 count, although responses are better as the count recovers. Live vaccines are contraindicated while the CD4 count is below 200; he is immune to measles, mumps, rubella, and varicella and needs none. [S]
  • Coronary artery disease and HIV (I25.10). HIV raises cardiovascular risk independently, which strengthens rather than changes the secondary-prevention plan: high-intensity atorvastatin with ezetimibe to an LDL below 55 mg/dL, aspirin, and blood pressure below 130/80. Integrase inhibitors can cause weight gain, which should be tracked. [S]
