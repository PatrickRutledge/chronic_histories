  • He wrote that he counted the steps in his walks and calculated the cubical contents of soup plates, coffee cups, and pieces of food, or his meal was unenjoyable, and that all repeated acts had to be divisible by three, and if he missed he felt impelled to do it all over again, even if it took hours.
