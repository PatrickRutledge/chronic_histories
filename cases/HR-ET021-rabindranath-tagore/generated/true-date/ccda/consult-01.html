Plan of Treatment

  • Start finasteride 5 mg daily now: with an 85 mL prostate it should shrink the gland by about a quarter over three to six months; double later PSA values for interpretation, with a new baseline at about six months. [S]
  • Continue tamsulosin 0.4 mg at bedtime; trial without catheter at about six weeks, once any infection is treated. [S]
  • Keep the indwelling urethral catheter, changed every four weeks, with closed drainage and no routine antibiotics; intermittent self-catheterization is the better option if he is willing and able, and a suprapubic catheter if long-term drainage is needed. [S]
  • If the trial fails, I would recommend an operation for refractory retention: laser enucleation (HoLEP) is the best choice for this size, under spinal or general anesthesia; transurethral resection or simple prostatectomy are alternatives. Prostatic artery embolization under local anesthesia is a less invasive option with lower and less durable success. Prostatic urethral lift and water vapor therapy are not well suited to this size, a median lobe, and retention. [S]
