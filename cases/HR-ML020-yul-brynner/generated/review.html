He gave other accounts himself: UPI reports that he said he was born on Sakhalin on July 11, 1915; a film reference gives July 12, 1915, as Taidje Kahn; TIME repeats the Sakhalin account.   3. Diagnosis and course. Sources agree that lung cancer was found in September 1983 and that the tumor could not be removed surgically (Cancer Today: “too close to his heart for surgery”).
