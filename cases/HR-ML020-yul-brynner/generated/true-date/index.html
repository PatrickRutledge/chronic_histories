Teaching objective: Work up hoarseness from left vocal fold paralysis in a 63-year-old former heavy smoker to a tissue diagnosis of advanced nonsquamous non-small cell lung cancer; stage it with PET-CT and brain MRI; obtain PD-L1 and comprehensive tissue and plasma biomarker testing before choosing first-line systemic therapy (pembrolizumab for PD-L1 70 percent with no actionable alteration); monitor for immune-related adverse events with TSH, free T4, liver tests, glucose, and cortisol as indicated, and evaluate a rash on treatment; start a bone-modifying agent safely; integrate palliative care early (ASCO 1974) with symptom and distress screening; record the tobacco history; begin advance care planning; and support his wish to keep performing, on the CMID lung cancer schedule and NCCN guidance.

Social, functional, and mental status

  • S Former smoker: about 3 packs a day from age 12 until he quit 12 years ago (roughly 115 pack-years); no tobacco, vaping, or cannabis since; wants other smokers to hear his story
  • S Alcohol: a glass of wine with dinner two or three nights a week (AUDIT-C 2); no other drug use
  • S Stage actor and singer, leading a national touring production with eight performances a week; has paused the tour for the tests and wants to return as soon as he safely can

Allergies

Medications

Procedures and immunizations

Procedures

  • S Flexible laryngoscopy by the ENT surgeon: left vocal fold immobile in the paramedian position with incomplete glottic closure; no laryngeal mass or mucosal lesion
  • S CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast: 5.2 cm left upper lobe mass invading the mediastinal fat of the aortopulmonary window and abutting the aortic arch and left pulmonary artery; enlarged aortopulmonary window (2.4 cm) and subcarinal (1.8 cm) lymph nodes; a small lytic lesion in the T10 vertebral body; no adrenal or liver lesion
  • S FDG PET-CT: avid left upper lobe mass (SUVmax 14) with mediastinal invasion; avid aortopulmonary window and subcarinal nodes (N2); no contralateral or supraclavicular nodes; avid lytic lesions at T10, the right ilium, and the left sixth rib, without fracture (M1c)
  • S MRI of the brain with contrast: no metastases
  • S Endobronchial ultrasound-guided needle aspiration of subcarinal (station 7) and left lower paratracheal (4L) nodes: adenocarcinoma, TTF-1 and napsin A positive; PD-L1 (22C3) tumor proportion score 70 percent; tissue sent for comprehensive next-generation sequencing (reported 12 days later: KRAS G12V and TP53 mutations; no EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF V600E, KRAS G12C, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, HER2, or NRG1 alteration), with concordant plasma cell-free DNA testing
  • S Outpatient palliative care visit: symptom assessment, coping, goals of care, and a plan for breathlessness and cough
  • S Office injection medialization of the left vocal fold under laryngoscopic guidance by the ENT surgeon, to improve glottic closure and voice
  • S Advance care planning conversation, about 30 minutes
