Plasma testing shows no actionable alteration, but a negative plasma result does not exclude a driver, so tissue sequencing (EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, KRAS G12C, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, HER2, NRG1) must be reviewed before an immune checkpoint inhibitor is started: starting one before a targetable driver is found can raise the toxicity of later targeted therapy and gives less benefit.
