Teaching objective: Re-entry primary care for a 65-year-old former physician and explorer establishing care four months after release from about six years in prison: medication reconciliation without records; high blood pressure at the first visit confirmed with home readings (stage 2 hypertension, two first-line drugs under the 2025 AHA/ACC guideline, target below 130/80); a right carotid bruit evaluated with duplex ultrasound, which finds an asymptomatic 50 to 69 percent internal carotid stenosis managed with best medical therapy (a statin, low-dose aspirin, blood pressure control and yearly surveillance) rather than revascularization, and stroke warning signs; a drug-induced gout flare on hydrochlorothiazide, treated with low-dose colchicine and followed by a switch to losartan under the 2020 ACR gout guideline; hepatitis C, latent tuberculosis, HIV and hepatitis B screening after incarceration (USPSTF and CDC); depression and shame screening; housing, income, benefits and reputation as health needs; and the Welcome to Medicare preventive examination with vaccinations. Built on the 2025 AHA/ACC hypertension guideline, the 2024 AHA/ASA primary stroke prevention guideline, the SVS 2021 and ESVS 2023 carotid guidelines, the 2018 AHA/ACC cholesterol guideline, the CMID hypertension, hyperlipidemia and stroke monographs, the 2020 ACR gout guideline, and the USPSTF and CDC recommendations.
