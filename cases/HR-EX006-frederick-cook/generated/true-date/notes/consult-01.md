## Assessment

Atherosclerotic, moderate and asymptomatic: he has had no stroke, transient ischemic attack or transient monocular blindness, and the examination is normal. The bruit led to the duplex, which is evaluation of a sign rather than population screening. The annual stroke risk with asymptomatic moderate stenosis on modern medical therapy is low, about 1 percent a year or less. Endarterectomy is considered for asymptomatic stenosis of 70 to 99 percent in suitable patients when the surgical stroke and death risk is below 3 percent, so revascularization is not indicated at 50 to 69 percent. Best medical therapy is the treatment. Brain imaging would not change management now. **S**
2. **Stage 2 hypertension** (I10). Confirmed by home readings. Lowering blood pressure is safe with a stenosis of this degree and is the most effective single measure against stroke; the target is below 130/80. **S**

## Recommendations

2. Atorvastatin 40 mg daily, with a lipid panel and ALT in 4 to 12 weeks; aim for at least a 50 percent LDL reduction and LDL below 70 mg/dL.
