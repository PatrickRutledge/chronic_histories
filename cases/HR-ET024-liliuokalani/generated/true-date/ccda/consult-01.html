Assessment

By the tissue-based definition this was a minor ischemic stroke, not a transient ischemic attack: the symptoms resolved, but the MRI shows an infarct in the hand area of the left motor cortex. A single small cortical infarct is embolic in pattern. The likeliest sources are the heart, particularly atrial fibrillation, given her age and a mildly enlarged left atrium, and less likely the left carotid plaque, which narrows the artery by less than 50%. Until a source is found, this is an embolic stroke of undetermined source. [S]

For a minor noncardioembolic stroke or high-risk TIA, aspirin with clopidogrel started within 24 hours and given for 21 days lowers early recurrence (CHANCE, POINT); hers was started at about 18 hours. Bleeding risk rises if it is continued longer, so after 21 days she should take a single antiplatelet, clopidogrel, unless atrial fibrillation is found. [S]
