## Historical basis

- After visiting her sister in Toronto at Christmas 1917, she moved there in February 1918 and worked in the Voluntary Aid Detachment at the Spadina Military Hospital, often on twelve-hour shifts, going wherever an extra pair of hands was needed. **D**
- During the 1918 influenza epidemic she worked nights on the ward that held the influenza patients; within weeks she fell ill herself, with severe sinus pain and terrible headaches.
