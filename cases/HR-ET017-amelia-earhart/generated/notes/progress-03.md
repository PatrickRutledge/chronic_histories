## History of present illness

Ms. Earhart is a 25-year-old private pilot who comes for her yearly preventive visit four weeks after her first visit for chronic sinus symptoms. Chronic rhinosinusitis without polyps was confirmed by nasal endoscopy two weeks ago, during a flare with pus on the left. She finished ten days of amoxicillin-clavulanate five days ago; the culture grew Haemophilus influenzae that makes beta-lactamase and is sensitive to amoxicillin-clavulanate. The discolored drainage has stopped and the left cheek pressure is less, but her nose is still blocked most days and her sense of smell has not come back. She rinses with saline twice daily and uses the fluticasone spray every morning, with no nosebleeds. She has not flown since the endoscopy, and she stopped the nighttime cold tablet. Her SNOT-22 today is 44, from 52 at the first visit and 58 during the flare. Nobody from the clinic called her at the end of the antibiotic course, as had been planned; she would have called if she had been worse. **S**
