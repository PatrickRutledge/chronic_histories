History of Present Illness

Ms. Earhart is a 25-year-old private pilot who comes for her yearly preventive visit four weeks after her first visit for chronic sinus symptoms. Nobody from the clinic called her at the end of the antibiotic course, as had been planned; she would have called if she had been worse. [S]
