Teaching objective: Pernicious anemia in an older man with old frostbite amputations: a 62-year-old retired naval officer and polar explorer comes in with four months of fatigue, breathlessness, pallor, a sore tongue and numb, unsteady feet. The episode covers the blood count, MCV, smear and reticulocytes; vitamin B12, methylmalonic acid, homocysteine and folate; iron studies; intrinsic factor and parietal cell antibodies and gastrin; treatment without delay when nerves are affected, with intramuscular cyanocobalamin loading, a weekly phase and lifelong monthly maintenance under the 2014 British Society for Haematology guideline and the 2024 NICE guideline, and high-dose oral therapy as an alternative; the reticulocyte response and the risk of low potassium early in treatment; restrictive transfusion; upper endoscopy for neuroendocrine tumors and gastric cancer risk and its surveillance under the 2021 AGA clinical practice update on atrophic gastritis; screening for autoimmune thyroid disease and diabetes; iron deficiency unmasked by recovery; the examination of subacute combined degeneration and its partial recovery; neuropathy and falls; and foot care for a man with old toe amputations, with footwear, daily skin checks and podiatry.
