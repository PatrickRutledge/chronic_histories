## Assessment and plan

Imaging and laboratory tests for the back are not indicated. Most episodes improve within a few weeks. **S**
   - Superficial heat for 20 minutes several times a day.
   - Stay active within the limits of pain and avoid bed rest; stand and walk for a few minutes every hour when traveling.
   - Acetaminophen 1 g up to three times a day as needed, no more than 3 g a day.
