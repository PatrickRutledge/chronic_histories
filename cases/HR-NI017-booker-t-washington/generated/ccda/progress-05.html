History of Present Illness

Mr. Washington is a 59-year-old man with hypertensive chronic kidney disease stage G3b A3 (eGFR 30 in June), controlled on amlodipine, losartan, dapagliflozin, and chlorthalidone, who asked for a same-day visit. Three days ago, while loading heavy boxes of books into a car before a speaking trip, he felt a sudden pull across the right lower back. He has had no vomiting or diarrhea and is eating and drinking normally. Sitting through a long car ride yesterday made the pain worse. [S]
