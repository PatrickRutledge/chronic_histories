Teaching objective: Hypertension, type 2 diabetes and kidney disease in a 49-year-old community organizer in the rural Mississippi Delta, managed at a community health center with trauma-informed care. The teaching contrast is her documented sterilization without consent: here every examination, test and procedure is explained beforehand, done only with her documented consent, with a chaperone of her choosing, and stopped or declined at her word without loss of care. The episode covers confirming hypertension with home readings under the 1966 AHA/ACC guideline, diagnosing and treating diabetes under the ADA Standards of Care 1967, kidney function and urine albumin with KDIGO, an ACE inhibitor and an SGLT2 inhibitor, a statin, retinal photographs, foot care, medication choices for cost and adherence, a community health worker on the team, a social needs screen with real resources for food, transportation and utility costs, shared decisions, and preventive care.
