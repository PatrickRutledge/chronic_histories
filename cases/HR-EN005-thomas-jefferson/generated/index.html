Teaching objective: Recurrent disabling headache in a working statesman: a 47-year-old cabinet secretary, seven weeks into a demanding new post, comes in a week into a bout of the headaches he has had since his twenties. The episode covers a structured headache history with a red-flag screen and no neuroimaging when none is indicated, a headache diary that confirms ICHD-3 migraine without aura, disability measured with the MIDAS, acute treatment with an NSAID and a triptan and the place of gepants and lasmiditan, why opioids and butalbital are avoided, counting acute-medicine days to prevent medication overuse headache, the criteria for preventive treatment and the choice among propranolol, topiramate, amitriptyline and a CGRP monoclonal antibody under the 2021 American Headache Society consensus, its 2024 position statement and the 2025 ACP guidelines, sleep, meals, caffeine and work stress as triggers, and an old badly healed wrist injury that makes writing slow and painful.

  • S Keeps careful daily account books and notebooks, and agreed at once to keep a headache diary

Medication overuse headache prevention: acute-medicine days counted in the diary, with limits of fewer than 10 days a month for a triptan or combination analgesic and fewer than 15 for an NSAID; never caffeine pills or combination productscompletedGL-ICHD3-2018, GL-AHS-2021
