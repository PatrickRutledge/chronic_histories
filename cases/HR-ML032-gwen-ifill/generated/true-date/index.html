Teaching objective: Evaluate postmenopausal bleeding at once, as any bleeding after menopause requires, in a 60-year-old Black woman, whose risk of dying of endometrial cancer is about twice that of White women because of later diagnosis, more aggressive non-endometrioid and p53-abnormal tumors, and delays in evaluating bleeding: transvaginal ultrasound together with endometrial sampling at the first evaluation (the ACOG update that no longer relies on a thin endometrium alone); histology with the molecular classification (POLE, mismatch repair, p53) that the FIGO 2013 staging uses; CT staging; gynecologic oncology care with minimally invasive hysterectomy and sentinel lymph node mapping; treatment chosen by stage and molecular class, here dostarlimab with carboplatin and paclitaxel for mismatch repair deficient stage IIIC2 disease, with immune-related adverse event monitoring; universal Lynch syndrome tumor screening with MLH1 promoter methylation testing and genetic counseling; and survivorship care, on the NCCN uterine neoplasms guideline, the ACOG postmenopausal bleeding guidance, the SGO surveillance statement and the CMID endometrial cancer monograph.
