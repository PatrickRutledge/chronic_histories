Social, functional, and mental status

Social history

  • S Occupation: senior national political leader with very long working days, late-night work, and frequent major speeches; on leave since the stroke
  • S Tobacco: smokes six to eight cigars a day, drawing on them without inhaling deeply and often letting them go out; lifelong habit; no cigarettes
  • S Alcohol: drinks every day: whisky well diluted with soda through the day, wine or champagne with dinner, and brandy afterwards; AUDIT-C 8 at intake
  • S Activity: little planned exercise; walks about the house and garden; afternoon nap most days
  • S Supports: married and lives with his spouse; adult children; a large, devoted staff

Functional status

  • S Walks with a stick outdoors; left hand clumsy with buttons and writing; independent in self-care with extra time; modified Rankin Scale about 2 at intake
  • S Walking without a stick, writing normally, and back at full work by about three months; modified Rankin Scale 1

Mental status

  • S Alert, articulate, and quick-witted; speech mildly slurred in long sentences at intake

Procedures and immunizations

Procedures

  • S MRI of the brain in the hospital: acute 12 mm lacunar infarct in the right internal capsule and corona radiata; chronic small-vessel white-matter changes in both hemispheres; no hemorrhage
  • S CT angiography of the head and neck in the hospital: no carotid, vertebral, or intracranial stenosis or occlusion
  • S Transthoracic echocardiography with bubble study in the hospital: normal left ventricular size and function, mild left ventricular hypertrophy, normal left atrium, no thrombus, no shunt
  • S 12-lead electrocardiogram: sinus rhythm 72/min, left ventricular hypertrophy by voltage, no atrial fibrillation
  • S 30-day ambulatory ECG monitoring: sinus rhythm throughout, occasional atrial ectopic beats, no atrial fibrillation or flutter
