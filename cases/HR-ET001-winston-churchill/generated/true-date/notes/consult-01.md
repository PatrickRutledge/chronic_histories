## Assessment

1. **Right-hemisphere lacunar infarct, recovering** (I69.354). A small-vessel lacunar infarct: a single small deep infarct with a classic motor syndrome, chronic small-vessel disease in both hemispheres, no large-artery stenosis, and no cardiac source on echocardiography. Hypertension is the main driver. No further vascular imaging is needed. Lacunar stroke carries a low yield for atrial fibrillation, but at 78 with left ventricular hypertrophy and earlier cerebrovascular events, 30 days of monitoring is reasonable because finding it would change treatment to anticoagulation. **S**
2. **Essential hypertension** (I10). 138/80 mmHg today, above the 130/80 target; the principal treatable risk factor. **S**
3. **Post-stroke emotionalism with mild depressive symptoms**. The sudden tearfulness out of proportion to his feelings is post-stroke emotionalism. He also has mild depressive symptoms (PHQ-9 8, no suicidal thoughts) that are improving as function returns. He does not meet criteria for a depressive disorder, and treatment is not needed now. **S**

## Recommendations

1. Aspirin has been stopped after 21 days; continue clopidogrel 75 mg daily long term.
2. Continue atorvastatin 80 mg; lipid panel 4 to 12 weeks after starting, aiming for LDL cholesterol below 70 mg/dL.
3. Lower blood pressure below 130/80 mmHg; I agree with adding lisinopril, with standing pressures checked because of his age.
4. 30-day ambulatory ECG monitoring, fitted today; if atrial fibrillation is found, change to an oral anticoagulant.
5. No further vascular imaging; repeat brain imaging only for new symptoms.
6. Continue physical and occupational therapy. Return to work gradually: half days from about six weeks, full days and major speeches by about three months, with rest before long speeches. Air travel is reasonable once he walks confidently outdoors.
7. Repeat the PHQ-9 at each primary care review; if it reaches 10 or more, or if the tearfulness becomes distressing, consider an SSRI.
8. Stop smoking cigars, and keep alcohol to no more than two drinks a day.
9. Repeat the MoCA within the first year.
