Judgment calls (dossier editorial resolutions)

  1. Index age and date shift. The episode is anchored on the documented stroke of 23 June 1953, when Churchill was 78. Synthetic date of birth 30 November 1947; shift 73 years. The modern stroke is dated June 23, 2026, so it maps to the documented date in the true frame. The index visit is July 1, 2026, eight days later (true frame 1 July 1953), inside the one-to-three-week window after discharge. Chronic-care visits follow on July 15, August 19, and October 7, 2026 (true frame the same days in 1953).
  2. Modern version of the documented stroke. The left hemiparesis, the drooping mouth, the slurred speech, the worsening over about three days, the lacunar interpretation, and the recovery are D. The modern hospital work-up (MRI, CT angiography, echocardiogram, telemetry), the treatment, the NIH Stroke Scale and other scores, the laboratory values, and the 30-day monitor are synthetic. He was not hospitalized in 1953; the modern episode does not describe the 1953 care. The documented blood pressures of 170/90 and 145/90 mmHg support the synthetic hypertension diagnosis but are not reused as visit values. The episode's goals of returning to full work and giving a major speech within about three months echo his documented Cabinet return in August and conference speech in October 1953; no decision or statement in the notes is attributed to his physicians, his family, or any other real person.
  3. Depression. The “black dog” is recorded only as claim CLM-0010, graded R and disputed, and is kept off the problem list. The documented despondency, apathy, and readiness to weep after the 1953 stroke (D) are echoed by synthetic post-stroke emotionalism and mild depressive symptoms. The synthetic PHQ-9 scores (8, 6, 4, and 3) stay below 10 with item 9 scored 0, so no depressive disorder is diagnosed and no F-code other than F17 is used; the safety-assessment rule therefore does not apply. The neurology consultant names an SSRI only as a contingency, and none was started.
  4. Alcohol and cigars. The documented daily drinking and cigar smoking (D, sensitive) are the basis for the synthetic modern history. AUDIT-C is used in the modern episode (8 at intake, 5 later), and the drinking is described as unhealthy alcohol use without an alcohol use disorder diagnosis, so no F10 code is used. Cigar smoking is coded F17.290. The roster's labels of alcohol use disorder, PTSD, and obesity were not verified in a fetched source and are not used; the synthetic weight gives a BMI of about 28.
  5. Atrial fibrillation. No atrial fibrillation is documented for 1953 in the fetched sources. The synthetic 30-day monitor is negative, so atrial fibrillation is not added.
  6. Past and family history. The 1941 chest pain is recorded as R with its disputed cardiac interpretation. The 1943 pneumonia, the 1949 stroke, and the 1950 to 1952 episodes are D. The father's 1895 death is recorded as R with the cause not established; the disputed syphilis theory is named only in the claim. The 1962 hip fracture and the final stroke fall after the index date and appear only in the timeline. The exact place and date of the October 1953 speech were not confirmed in a fetched source and are not used.
  7. Sick and wellness visits. An unscheduled sick visit on July 29, 2026 (true frame 29 July 1953) treats orthostatic hypotension after lisinopril was added to amlodipine 10 mg in hot weather. It was chosen because blood pressure lowering after stroke in an older patient must be balanced against orthostatic hypotension and falls, and because the visit has to exclude a transient ischemic attack; it does not echo a documented illness. The basic metabolic panel due one to two weeks after starting lisinopril was drawn at that visit. The subsequent annual wellness visit on October 28, 2026 (true frame 28 October 1953) repeats the MoCA and PHQ-9 and gives the influenza vaccine. Its advance care planning names no real person as his health care agent.
  8. Focused visits. The three chronic-care follow-ups (two weeks, seven weeks, and fourteen weeks after intake) are documented as focused visits on the CMID stroke/TIA monograph, with the hypertension monograph for laboratory monitoring and the depression monograph for measurement-based mood screening. One protocol deviation is recorded as deferred instead of inventing a result: obstructive sleep apnea evaluation, which he declined at the eight-week visit (STOP-Bang 3, no symptoms). The modified Rankin Scale was recorded at about fifteen weeks, the scheduled review nearest to 90 days.
  9. Consultation. The neurology consultation on July 15, 2026, is synthetic.
