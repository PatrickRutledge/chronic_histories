## Assessment and plan

1. **Acute viral upper respiratory infection** (J06.9). A cold caught from his students: a short illness with a sore throat and runny nose before the cough, a normal chest examination, and continued weight gain. This is not tuberculosis treatment failure, which would bring back cough with sputum, night sweats, weight loss, or hemoptysis over weeks, and his April cultures were negative. Antibiotics would not help. Zinc lozenges and mineral-containing cold remedies bind moxifloxacin and lower its absorption, and an antibiotic such as azithromycin would add to the QT effect of moxifloxacin. **S**
   - Fluids, honey, rest, and saline nose spray; acetaminophen for throat pain or fever.
