  • His biographer Martin Duberman describes his first breakdown in 1955, after health problems related to prostate surgery: a period of intense, driven energy that was followed by a deep depression. [D]
  • A writer reviewing the Duberman biography has argued that he had the classic symptoms of bipolar disorder; no formal diagnosis of bipolar disorder in his lifetime is quoted. [R]
