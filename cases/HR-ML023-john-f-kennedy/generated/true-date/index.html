Teaching objective: Primary care for a 30-year-old legislator three months after primary adrenal insufficiency was diagnosed during an adrenal crisis abroad, on the Endocrine Society 1938 guideline: confirming the cause with 21-hydroxylase antibodies without stopping treatment, bringing hydrocortisone from a discharge dose down to 15 to 25 mg a day in divided doses and adjusting fludrocortisone by blood pressure lying and standing, sodium, potassium and renin, written sick-day (stress-dose) rules taught and then used in an intercurrent febrile illness, an emergency injection kit and medical ID, screening for associated autoimmune disease (thyroid, vitamin B12, celiac disease, diabetes) with a raised TSH confirmed before treatment and levothyroxine started only once glucocorticoid replacement is secure (CMID hypothyroidism monograph), bone health after earlier corticosteroid exposure, and exercise-based care of chronic low back pain after disc surgery on the ACP guideline, with his privacy about the diagnosis respected.
