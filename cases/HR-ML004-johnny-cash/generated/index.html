Teaching objective: Recognize that orthostatic lightheadedness, leg numbness, and falls in an older adult with long-standing type 2 diabetes fit diabetic autonomic and peripheral neuropathy rather than a prior label of Shy-Drager syndrome; set an individualized HbA1c goal, choose glucose-lowering drugs that avoid hypoglycemia, treat neurogenic orthostatic hypotension, and coordinate glaucoma care so that eye drops do not worsen blood pressure, using the CMID diabetes and glaucoma schedules.
