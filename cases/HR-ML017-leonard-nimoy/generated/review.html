Judgment calls (dossier editorial resolutions)

  1. Recent-death rule. Nimoy died in 2015. The case uses only what he made public himself (his posts announcing COPD, describing his limits, and urging smokers to quit, and his description of himself as a former heavy smoker), as reported by ABC News, TODAY, UPI, and the Associated Press, together with basic biographical facts and the cause of death as reported publicly. No medical records, physician statements, family accounts, or speculation about undisclosed conditions are used. His spirometry, GOLD grade and group, scores, inhaler, rehabilitation, oxygen assessment, laboratory results, vaccines, and preventive care are synthetic. Claims about his smoking are marked sensitive. The recent-death review rule applies.
  2. Date of the announcement. ABC News dates the first post January 29, 2014; TODAY dates it January 30. The case says late January. The quit year of about 1984 is derived from “30 yrs ago” and is approximate.
  3. Index age. The modern episode presents Nimoy at 82, two weeks after his public announcement. The synthetic date of birth is March 26, 1943 (shift 12 years). The index visit on February 10, 2026 maps to February 10, 2014; the chronic-care visits on March 10, May 12, and August 11, 2026 map to the same days of 2014; and the annual wellness visit on October 6, 2026 maps to October 6, 2014, when he was 83. Every visit falls more than four months before his death. Past medical history includes only the smoking history and the COPD he disclosed before the index date; his later posts and his death are claims only.
  4. Synthetic clinical picture. The breathlessness that stops him after about 100 meters (mMRC 3) and the wheelchair for long distances follow his public statement that he could not walk distances. Everything else is synthetic and was chosen to stay modest: no exacerbation, hospital stay, or oxygen use is recorded, so nothing reads as a claim about his private course; resting saturation stays at 93 to 94 percent; alpha-1 antitrypsin, hemoglobin, creatinine, and the ECG are normal; mood and cognitive screens are negative. The pack-year figure (about 40) is synthetic; no fetched source quantifies his smoking. His height (185 cm) and weight are synthetic. His past alcohol use was not researched and is not used; the social history records no current alcohol use, as a synthetic entry. The CAT, mMRC grade, GAD-2, and Timed Up and Go have no verified LOINC code on the terminology server and are recorded as narrative.
  5. Annual wellness visit. A subsequent Medicare annual wellness visit (age 83, so `annual_wellness_subsequent`) was added on October 6, 2026 (true frame October 6, 2014), eight weeks after the six-month review. He has been eligible for Medicare for many years, and the visit notes a previous annual wellness visit with his earlier clinician more than a year before, so the subsequent visit type applies. No sick visit was added: under the recent-death rule the case adds no synthetic illness episode. Judgment calls: (1) the advance care planning names an unnamed health care agent and records only general priorities (staying at home, his creative work, being told plainly), with no treatment preference, because the true-frame date is months before his death and a preference could read as a claim about his real choices; (2) lung cancer screening is not offered because he quit more than 15 years ago (and is over 80), which is the teaching point; (3) the aneurysm screen is not offered because he is past 75, and colorectal and prostate screening are not continued at 83; (4) a DXA scan and audiology are ordered with no results recorded; (5) the spouse is unnamed and gives no statement.
  6. Focused visits. The three chronic-care follow-ups (four weeks, three months, and six months after intake) are documented as focused visits on the CMID COPD monograph, with GOLD 2025 and the ATS rehabilitation and oxygen guidelines, and each lists every item the protocol calls for at that point, with its interval and status. Protocol deviation recorded instead of an invented result: the PCV20 dose planned at four weeks was not given because the vaccine was out of stock and was given nine weeks later at the three-month visit, with no harm and a corrective action. Yearly spirometry and mood screening are marked up to date or due, not repeated early.
