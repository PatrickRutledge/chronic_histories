Teaching objective: Diagnose and treat sarcoidosis with cardiac involvement in a 52-year-old Black woman and touring singer, in whom sarcoidosis is more common and more often severe and fatal than in White Americans, on the ATS 2020 diagnosis guideline, the ERS 2021 treatment guideline, the HRS 2014 cardiac sarcoidosis consensus and the AHA 2024 scientific statement: chest imaging with bilateral hilar adenopathy; pulmonary function with DLCO; EBUS-guided lymph node aspiration showing non-necrotizing granulomas, with tuberculosis and fungal infection excluded; screening every patient for other organ involvement (eye examination, ECG, calcium and vitamin D handling, liver and kidney tests); cardiac evaluation with ECG, Holter monitoring, echocardiography, cardiac MRI and FDG-PET before treatment begins; prednisone with methotrexate as a steroid-sparing agent, with methotrexate safety monitoring; bone protection that avoids calcium supplements while calcium is high; pneumocystis prophylaxis, vaccination and tuberculosis screening before immunosuppression; arrhythmia risk stratification with electrophysiology study and a defibrillator; and a plan that protects her voice, her performing and her travel.
