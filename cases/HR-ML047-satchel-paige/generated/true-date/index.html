Teaching objective: COPD in an older Black man, with attention to equity: a 73-year-old retired pitcher and former smoker comes in with two years of breathlessness on exertion and a morning cough. The episode covers the differential of breathlessness (COPD, heart failure, coronary disease, anemia), with NT-proBNP and an ECG; spirometry with a post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC below 0.70 interpreted with the race-neutral GLI Global equations under the 1976 American Thoracic Society statement, and how race-specific equations would have understated his impairment; the CAT and mMRC, exacerbation history and GOLD 1978 group; LAMA with LABA as first maintenance therapy and the blood eosinophil count that argues against an inhaled corticosteroid; inhaler technique for a dry powder device; pulmonary rehabilitation under the 1976 ATS guideline; one-time alpha-1 antitrypsin testing; pulse oximetry that can overestimate saturation in darker skin, with an arterial blood gas, a six-minute walk, and the LOTT trial and 1973 ATS guideline for long-term and ambulatory oxygen; vaccination (influenza, pneumococcal, RSV, COVID-19, Tdap, zoster); lung cancer screening eligibility; cardiovascular risk and a statin; a moderate exacerbation treated with prednisone and an antibiotic; a written and spoken action plan; and advance care planning.

Social, functional, and mental status

Social history

  • S Retired professional pitcher who starred for two decades in the Negro leagues, was kept out of the major leagues until 42 by the exclusion of Black players, pitched in the majors into his late fifties, and is in the Hall of Fame; still speaks at schools and community events
  • S Lives in Kansas City with his wife; seven grown children, several nearby
  • S Synthetic smoking history: smoked cigarettes from about 20, roughly three-quarters of a pack a day, about 30 pack-years, and stopped on his own eleven years ago; no vaping; no cannabis. A beer on a holiday (AUDIT-C 1)
  • S Walks to the corner store and back most days, stopping twice to catch his breath; used to fish; no stairs at home except the porch
  • S Grew up poor in segregated Mobile and spent five years of his boyhood in a segregated reform school; long experience of being barred from hotels and restaurants on the road; distrustful of being judged by race, and wants his care explained and his numbers shown to him
  • S Strengths: extraordinary physical discipline over a very long career, humor, a gift for storytelling, a close family and wide community respect

Functional status

  • S Independent in daily activities; breathless after one flight of stairs or a block on the level (mMRC 2); has cut back on fishing trips and on speaking engagements that involve walking

Mental status

  • S Alert, witty and guarded at first; mood good but frustrated at being slowed; PHQ-9 5 with item 9 scored 0; no thoughts of self-harm

Allergies and medications

Allergies

  • S No known allergy

Medications

  • S Albuterol 90 micrograms per inhalation metered-dose inhaler with a spacer, two inhalations every four hours as needed for breathlessness
  • S Umeclidinium 62.5 micrograms with vilanterol 25 micrograms dry powder inhaler (a LAMA with a LABA), one inhalation once a day

Procedures and immunizations

Procedures

  • S 12-lead ECG: sinus rhythm at 82; normal axis; no Q waves, ST changes or signs of right ventricular strain; P waves normal
  • S Spirometry before and after 400 micrograms of albuterol, meeting ATS/ERS acceptability and repeatability standards, interpreted with the GLI Global race-neutral reference equations: post-bronchodilator FEV1 2.02 L (61% predicted), FVC 3.48 L, FEV1/FVC 0.58; a 0.10 L (5%) bronchodilator response
  • S Low-dose CT of the chest for lung cancer screening: centrilobular emphysema, worst in the upper lobes; a 4 mm solid nodule in the right lower lobe (Lung-RADS 2, benign appearance); moderate coronary artery calcification; no mass or enlarged nodes
  • S Outpatient pulmonary rehabilitation, twice a week for ten weeks (20 sessions): endurance and strength training, breathing techniques and education
  • S Chest X-ray, posteroanterior and lateral: hyperinflated lungs with flattened diaphragms; no consolidation, effusion, pneumothorax or enlarged heart
  • S Advance care planning conversation, about 25 minutes, with his consent
  • S Ultrasound of the abdominal aorta for aneurysm screening: maximum diameter 2.4 cm; no aneurysm
  • S 12-lead ECG after the exacerbation: sinus rhythm at 76; unchanged from intake; no ischemic changes, arrhythmia or right heart strain
