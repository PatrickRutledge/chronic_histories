## Assessment and plan

1. **Acute low back strain** (S39.012A). Mechanical right lower back pain after lifting, reproduced by movement and paraspinal pressure, without red flags for fracture, infection, cancer, or nerve compression. New back pain in a patient with an aneurysm must first be weighed as possible aneurysm pain: here the aneurysm is non-tender, his blood pressure and pulse are stable, he has no fainting or abdominal pain, and the same-day duplex ultrasound shows no change in size and no fluid around the aorta. A symptomatic aneurysm is therefore not suspected, and no CT is needed today. **S**
   - Stay active, with short walks; avoid heavy lifting and twisting for two weeks.
   - Heat to the back; acetaminophen 1 g up to three times a day as needed, no more than 3 g a day.
   - Avoid ibuprofen, naproxen, and other anti-inflammatory pain medicines, which add bleeding risk to aspirin and can harm the kidneys with losartan; avoid muscle relaxants, which cause drowsiness and falls at his age.
   - Expect improvement within one to two weeks.
