The CT angiography two weeks ago showed a 5.2 cm fusiform infrarenal aneurysm with a 2.2 cm neck, calcified atheroma of the aorta and iliac arteries, thickening along the front of the aneurysm from the old wrapping, and no fluid around the aorta.
