Teaching objective: First six months of care of a 70-year-old man with an infrarenal abdominal aortic aneurysm below the repair threshold, known since an exploratory laparotomy at which it was wrapped rather than repaired: baseline duplex ultrasound and CT angiography, surveillance every six months at 5.0 to 5.4 cm, blood pressure below 130/80, a high-intensity statin, low-dose aspirin for aortic atheroma, smoking cessation with nicotine replacement, avoidance of fluoroquinolones, education about symptoms that need emergency care, and a documented shared decision with vascular surgery about elective repair thresholds (5.5 cm in men, rapid growth, or symptoms) and endovascular versus open repair, following the Society for Vascular Surgery 2018 and ACC/AHA 2022 guidelines with the CMID hypertension and hyperlipidemia monographs. His preferences are recorded as synthetic shared decision-making; the decision he made at the end of his life is history only.
