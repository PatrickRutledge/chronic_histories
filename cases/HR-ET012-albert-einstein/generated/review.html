Judgment calls (dossier editorial resolutions)

  1. The aneurysm and the 1948 operation. The abdominal pain of 1948, the exploratory laparotomy of December 31, 1948, the aneurysm found and wrapped in cellophane, and his discharge about three weeks later are documented (D) and appear as past medical history. Source 3 dates his seeking advice to the fall of 1948 without giving the operation date; source 2’s date is used. The documented size (“the size of a grapefruit”) is not used: the modern episode is a synthetic aneurysm below the repair threshold (5.0 cm by ultrasound, 5.2 cm by CT, 5.1 cm at six months), as the teaching objective requires. Modern notes refer to the operation without a date and record that the wrapping does not change the thresholds or surveillance. The CT finding of thickening along the front of the aneurysm is a synthetic depiction of the documented wrapping.
  2. Index age and dates. The modern episode presents Einstein at 70, three months after the operation. The synthetic date of birth is March 14, 1956 (shift 77 years). The index visit on March 24, 2026 maps to March 24, 1949; the chronic-care visits on April 21, July 14, and September 22, 2026, the sick visit on June 2, 2026, and the annual wellness visit on August 25, 2026 map to the same days of 1949. Source 2 says he left the hospital for a long holiday in Florida; it does not give its length, so the visits are not checked against it.
  3. Death and his refusal of surgery. The rupture in April 1955, the surgeon’s advice, his refusal of operation, and his death are history only (D), recorded as claims dated after the episode and absent from intake documents. Source 2’s introduction gives April 15, 1955 as the date of death, which conflicts with its own narrative and with source 1; April 18 is preferred and April 15 is recorded as a conflicting alternative. His reported words are paraphrased, not quoted. The modern episode does not stage his death: it records synthetic shared decision-making in which he chooses surveillance now, would consider endovascular repair at the threshold, wants each decision made with him when the time comes, and has set no treatment limits.
  4. Pipe smoking. The quantity, readiness to quit, quit date, nicotine replacement, and abstinence are synthetic. The habit of holding an empty, unlit pipe after quitting echoes the documented later habit (source 4) without claiming it happened in 1949. Lung cancer screening is recorded as not indicated because the USPSTF criteria count cigarette pack-years.
