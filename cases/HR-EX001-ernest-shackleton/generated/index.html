HR-EX001-SYN

Teaching objective: Primary care and cardiology for a 47-year-old expedition leader and heavy smoker with new exertional chest tightness and breathlessness, four months before leading a ship-based polar expedition: pretest probability and risk assessment, a resting ECG and risk-factor tests, coronary CT angiography with CT-FFR as the first test, an echocardiogram, guideline-directed medical therapy (aspirin, high-intensity statin with ezetimibe to an LDL below 55 mg/dL, a beta-blocker, and sublingual nitroglycerin), revascularization when angina persists despite medical therapy and what it does and does not offer in chronic coronary disease, dual antiplatelet therapy, cardiac rehabilitation, varenicline with counseling, the AUDIT with a brief intervention, prediabetes, and fitness for remote high-latitude travel: a symptom-limited exercise test, an evacuation plan, a defibrillator and medicines on board, and cold stress. His reluctance to be examined, and to let anything stop the expedition, is worked through with shared decision-making. Built on the 2023 AHA/ACC chronic coronary disease guideline, the 2024 ESC chronic coronary syndromes guideline, and the CMID ischemic heart disease and hyperlipidemia monographs.

  • S Married; lives in London while he organizes the expedition, working long days on finance, the ship, stores, and the crew
  • S Tobacco: about 25 cigarettes a day since 16 (about 39 pack-years); has never tried to stop; first cigarette within 30 minutes of waking
