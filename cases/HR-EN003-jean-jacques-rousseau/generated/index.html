HR-EN003-SYN

Teaching objective: Assess a 54-year-old writer with long-held beliefs that former friends are plotting against him, poor sleep, and lifelong difficulty passing urine. Engage him without arguing with or endorsing the beliefs; exclude medical, substance, and neurocognitive causes; distinguish delusional disorder, persecutory type, from paranoid personality disorder, schizophrenia, and a mood disorder with psychotic features (DSM-5-TR); assess the risk of harm to himself and to others at every visit; offer cognitive behavioral therapy for psychosis and an antipsychotic by shared decision-making, accepting a refusal and offering again; and, if he starts one, choose a drug with little anticholinergic effect and follow the metabolic monitoring schedule. In parallel, evaluate chronic incomplete emptying with uroflowmetry, post-void residual, kidney function and ultrasound, retrograde urethrography, and cystoscopy; stop self-dilation; and offer urethroplasty instead of further dilation for a stricture that has recurred for decades (AUA 2023), with his capacity to consent assessed, not assumed absent because of the delusional beliefs. The teaching note contrasts this with his documented lifelong self-treatment with bougies and the disputed causes of his illness.
