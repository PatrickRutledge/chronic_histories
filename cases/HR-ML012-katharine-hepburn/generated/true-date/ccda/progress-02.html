History of Present Illness

Ms. Hepburn is a 72-year-old woman with a shaky hand she has noticed for several years and a head tremor that has been visible for at least a year and has slowly worsened, who returns four weeks after her first visit and is seen by movement disorders neurology on the same day. She notices the hand tremor most when carrying a full cup, pouring, writing letters, and signing her name; she now carries cups half full. The head tremor bothers others more than her, and she finds it settles when she rests her head against a high-backed chair. Her voice wavers on sustained notes on stage. She has had no stiffness, slowness, shuffling, falls, numbness, or change in memory. She takes no medicines and has one cup of coffee in the morning. She says her grandfather's head and hand shook too, and she wants it known that this is not Parkinson's disease. Her thyroid test and blood tests from the first visit were normal. [S]
