Teaching objective: Evaluation and treatment of essential tremor in a 72-year-old woman with a visible head tremor, a mild voice tremor, and action tremor of both hands: diagnosis by the MDS consensus classification and distinction from Parkinson's disease, exclusion of thyroid and drug causes, a standard tremor rating with spiral drawing and handwriting at every visit, propranolol with heart rate, blood pressure, asthma, mood, fatigue, and fall checks, primidone as the alternative with its sedation and fall risks at her age, voice therapy, occupational therapy for adaptive aids, botulinum toxin for head and voice tremor, and the criteria for referral for focused ultrasound or deep brain stimulation, on the AAN essential tremor guidelines with CMID general items for older adults. The Parkinson's disease label that some accounts gave her tremor is recorded as a contested retrospective interpretation, not a diagnosis.

Functional status

  • S At the first visit: TETRAS performance subscale 22 of 64 and activities of daily living subscale 14 of 48. Side-to-side (no-no) head tremor of moderate amplitude, present when sitting and standing and lessened when the head is supported; wavering pitch on a sustained vowel; postural and kinetic tremor of both hands, right slightly more than left, on finger-to-nose testing and when pouring, which she has noticed for several years; spiral drawing with tremor waves; handwriting legible but shaky. No rest tremor, bradykinesia, rigidity, dystonic posturing, or ataxia; normal tandem gait
  • S Falls: none in the past year; Timed Up and Go 9 seconds
