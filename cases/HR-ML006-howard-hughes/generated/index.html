Teaching objective: Obsessive-compulsive disorder in a wealthy and powerful 41-year-old aviator and businessman with chronic pain after a near-fatal plane crash and long-term codeine use. The episode covers a Y-BOCS severity rating, DSM-5-TR criteria with the insight specifier and the differential (obsessive-compulsive personality, illness anxiety, post-traumatic stress after the crash, psychosis); a structured safety assessment with the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline; exposure and response prevention as first-line therapy with an SSRI (sertraline, raised to 200 mg) at OCD doses under the APA 2007 guideline and its 2013 watch and NICE CG31, with augmentation options held in reserve; an opioid review under the CDC 2022 guideline with a prescription drug monitoring program check, urine drug testing, naloxone, a nonjudgmental DSM-5-TR assessment that finds opioid use disorder and a switch from codeine to buprenorphine, which treats both the disorder and the pain; non-opioid pain care with physical therapy; audiometry and hearing aids for noise-induced hearing loss; weight, nutrition and kidney function; and how money and household staff can carry out compulsions for a patient and so feed avoidance, with staff involved in treatment only with his consent.
