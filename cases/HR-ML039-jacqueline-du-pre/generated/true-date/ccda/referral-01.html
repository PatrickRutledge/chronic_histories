  • In 1971 her playing declined as she began to lose sensitivity in her fingers and other parts of her body; she returned to England exhausted, took a sabbatical from 1971 to 1972 and performed only rarely. When she resumed in 1972 the numbness in her fingers worsened, with fatigue. [D]
