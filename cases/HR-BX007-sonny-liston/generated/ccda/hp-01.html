His birth date is uncertain: there was no birth certificate (Arkansas did not require them until 1965); he filed for one in 1953 giving May 8, 1932, which the Encyclopedia of Arkansas and the Scribner encyclopedia use with a question; his family's 1930 census record does not list him, so he was probably born no earlier than late 1930; his mother gave several dates; and other sources suggest 1927 or 1928. [D]

  • He had virtually no schooling and never learned to read or write. [D]
  • At thirteen he followed his mother to St. Louis. Arrested in 1950 after a series of robberies, he was sentenced to five years in the Missouri State Penitentiary in Jefferson City, where a Catholic chaplain encouraged him to box; he was paroled in 1952. [D]
  • He turned professional in September 1953 and broke his jaw in a 1954 loss. He won the world heavyweight title in Chicago on September 25, 1962, with a first-round knockout, and lost it in Miami Beach on February 25, 1964, when he did not come out for the seventh round, citing a shoulder injury; he lost the rematch in 1965. His record was 50 wins (39 by knockout) and 4 losses; he moved to Las Vegas in 1966. [D]
  • He was described as a heavy drinker and a reckless driver with frequent arrests. [D]
