Teaching objective: Recognize new distortion in one eye as a possible sign of neovascular age-related macular degeneration that needs ophthalmology evaluation within days; follow intravitreal anti-VEGF treatment with acuity, OCT, and pressure checks under the AAO Preferred Practice Pattern; protect the fellow eye with AREDS2 supplements and home Amsler grid monitoring; and support function, safety, and mood with low-vision rehabilitation, in an artist whose central vision was lost before these treatments existed.
