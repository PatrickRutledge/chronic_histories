Teaching objective: Confirm asthma in a 72-year-old Black woman with years of wheeze and breathlessness who has been treated with albuterol alone, and tell it apart from COPD and heart failure: spirometry with bronchodilator response interpreted with race-neutral equations, DLCO, FeNO and blood eosinophils, chest X-ray, BNP and echocardiography. Then treat on GINA 1947 track 1 (budesonide-formoterol as maintenance and reliever, with SABA-only treatment stopped) and the 1942 NAEPP focused update, check inhaler technique and adherence before any step-up, write an asthma action plan, manage an exacerbation and review after it, assess for biologic therapy against the definition of severe asthma, and treat the comorbidities that worsen asthma (chronic rhinosinusitis, reflux, obesity without stigma) and the hypertensive heart disease found on the echocardiogram, with vaccinations; Black Americans have higher asthma death rates and race-based spirometry equations have hidden lung impairment, so each step is checked for under-treatment.
