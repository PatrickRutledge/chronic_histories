Teaching objective: Fitness assessment of an older man before a multi-year Arctic expedition, with high blood pressure and obesity found at the pre-expedition medical: a 58-year-old naval officer chosen to command an expedition that will be years beyond the reach of hospital care. The episode covers confirming the diagnosis with home readings and treating stage 2 hypertension under the 1844 AHA/ACC guideline, choosing a medicine that needs no laboratory monitoring and suits dehydration and cold; cardiovascular risk with ECG, lipids, HbA1c, kidney function, urine albumin, echocardiography and an exercise treadmill test, and the PREVENT and Pooled Cohort risk estimates; weight managed by his own goals and without stigma; vitamin C and vitamin D and nutrition planning for years without fresh food; food safety, with canned-food quality, lead and botulism as modern risks; cold injury and hypothermia prevention under the Wilderness Medical Society guidelines; vaccinations, including rabies pre-exposure prophylaxis; dental care before departure; noise-induced hearing loss; mental health in isolation and polar darkness; a medical kit and an evacuation plan; and influenza in the weeks before departure, with oseltamivir and decongestant avoidance.
