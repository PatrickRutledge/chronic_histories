Teaching objective: Evaluate chronic abdominal pain, diarrhea, and weight loss in a young woman of 21: confirm Crohn disease by ileocolonoscopy with biopsies and MR enterography, and exclude intestinal tuberculosis before any immunosuppression (interferon-gamma release assay, chest X-ray, and biopsies for acid-fast bacilli, TB PCR, and culture); classify the disease (Montreal) and its risk; start an advanced therapy early for moderate to severe disease, with latent tuberculosis, hepatitis B, and vaccine preparation first; correct iron, vitamin D, and nutrition; protect bone; treat to target with CRP, fecal calprotectin, and endoscopy; plan contraception, fertility, and pregnancy; screen mood; and help her keep composing. The historical illness is graded R: called intestinal tuberculosis in her time and proposed to be Crohn disease since, it is not settled.
