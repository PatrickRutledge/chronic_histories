Assessment

  • Hereditary hemochromatosis, C282Y homozygous (E83.110). C282Y homozygosity with a transferrin saturation over 45% and a raised ferritin confirms HFE hemochromatosis. Alcohol raises ferritin and adds liver injury, but with this genotype and a saturation of 76% most of the iron excess is genetic; the liver iron concentration on MRI will show the size of the load. His heavy drinking multiplies the risk of cirrhosis from iron. [S]
  • Liver staging (E83.110). A ferritin over 1,000 ng/mL with a raised ALT means a higher risk of advanced fibrosis, so staging is needed. Today's stiffness of 9.4 kPa is indeterminate and may be raised by recent drinking and inflammation. An MRI with R2* liver iron quantification and magnetic resonance elastography can stage the liver without a biopsy, as the ACG guideline allows; a biopsy would be considered only if the MRI suggested advanced fibrosis or another cause. [S]
