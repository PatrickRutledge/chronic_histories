Teaching objective: Primary care, gynecologic oncology and early palliative care for a 35-year-old mathematician with five months of abnormal bleeding and pelvic pain who has taken prescribed opium tincture for years: speculum examination and biopsy of a visible cervical tumor at the first visit, with tumor HPV typing; intravenous iron for iron deficiency anemia from blood loss; conversion of an unmeasured opium tincture to measured oral morphine, then long-acting morphine, with naloxone, a bowel regimen, safe storage and a prescription monitoring check, applying the ASCO 2023 guideline on opioids for cancer pain and noting that the CDC 2022 opioid guideline does not cover cancer pain; FIGO 2018 staging with MRI and PET-CT and biopsy of a lung metastasis (stage IVB); tumor biomarkers (PD-L1, mismatch repair, HER2) and why germline testing is not indicated; palliative pelvic radiation for bleeding; first-line carboplatin, paclitaxel and pembrolizumab under the NCCN cervical cancer guideline and KEYNOTE-826, without bevacizumab while she was bleeding after pelvic radiation; specialist palliative care from diagnosis (ASCO 2024); immune-related thyroid monitoring; opioid-induced constipation; goals-of-care conversations and advance care planning; vaccination of an adult on chemotherapy; and protecting clear-headed hours for her mathematical work.
