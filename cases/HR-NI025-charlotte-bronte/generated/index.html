Teaching objective: Prenatal care of a first pregnancy at 38 complicated by hyperemesis gravidarum, following ACOG Practice Bulletin 189 and RCOG Green-top Guideline 69 (2024): dating ultrasound; severity by the PUQE-24 score, weight, and hydration rather than urine ketones; electrolytes, magnesium, phosphate, liver tests, and thyroid tests with recognition of gestational transient thyrotoxicosis; stepwise antiemetics (doxylamine-pyridoxine, then metoclopramide, then ondansetron after counseling on the very small absolute risk of orofacial clefting); admission with intravenous saline and potassium, thiamine before any dextrose to prevent Wernicke encephalopathy, magnesium replacement, refeeding precautions, and weight-adjusted thromboprophylaxis; mental health screening with the PHQ-9 and EPDS; routine prenatal care at an advanced maternal age, with aneuploidy screening offered (cell-free DNA and nuchal translucency, with diagnostic testing available), low-dose aspirin for two moderate preeclampsia risk factors, and tuberculosis screening with an interferon-gamma release assay because of tuberculosis deaths in her household. The historical cause of her death is disputed and is recorded as conflicting retrospective claims, not decided by the modern episode.
