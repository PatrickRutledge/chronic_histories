Plan of Treatment

  • First prenatal visit: dating ultrasound; blood count, blood group and antibody screen, rubella immunity, hepatitis B surface antigen, HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis C tests; urine culture; metabolic panel and urinalysis because of vomiting; influenza vaccine; folic acid 0.4 mg daily in place of the iron-containing prenatal vitamin while nausea lasts. Guideline: USPSTF A and B Recommendations (2026); Guidelines for Vaccinating Pregnant Persons (2026) [S]
  • Nausea and vomiting, step by step: small frequent dry meals, fluids between meals, ginger if she wishes, and avoiding smells that trigger nausea; doxylamine-pyridoxine from today, raised to four tablets a day if needed; then a second drug (metoclopramide, promethazine, or ondansetron after counseling); PUQE-24 score, weight, pulse, blood pressure, and electrolytes at each visit; same-day review if she cannot keep fluids down for 24 hours or loses more than 5 percent of her pre-pregnancy weight. Guideline: ACOG Practice Bulletin No. 189: Nausea and Vomiting of Pregnancy (2018); The Management of Nausea and Vomiting in Pregnancy and Hyperemesis Gravidarum (Green-top Guideline No. 69) (2024) [S]
  • Aneuploidy screening offered to all, with diagnostic testing available: cell-free DNA from 10 weeks, a nuchal translucency scan at 11 to 14 weeks, or chorionic villus sampling or amniocentesis; a detailed anatomy scan at 18 to 22 weeks. Guideline: Screening for Fetal Chromosomal Abnormalities: ACOG Practice Bulletin, Number 226 (2020) [S]
  • Tuberculosis screening with an interferon-gamma release assay because of tuberculosis deaths in her household; a shielded chest radiograph and treatment decisions only if it is positive. Guideline: Latent Tuberculosis Infection in Adults: Screening (2023) [S]
  • Mental health: PHQ-9 at intake and the EPDS in each trimester and after any admission, with support for the strain of severe vomiting and for anxiety rooted in family loss. Guideline: Screening and Diagnosis of Mental Health Conditions During Pregnancy and Postpartum (Clinical Practice Guideline No. 4) (2023); Perinatal Depression: Preventive Interventions (2019) [S]
  • Preeclampsia prevention: low-dose aspirin 81 mg at bedtime from 12 to 16 weeks until delivery if she has one high-risk or more than one moderate-risk factor (she has two: first pregnancy and age 35 or older); blood pressure at every visit. Guideline: Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication (2021) [S]

Guideline: ACOG Practice Bulletin No. 189: Nausea and Vomiting of Pregnancy (2018); The Management of Nausea and Vomiting in Pregnancy and Hyperemesis Gravidarum (Green-top Guideline No. 69) (2024) [S]
