History of Present Illness

Mr. Charles is a 65-year-old man, blind since childhood, with an opioid use disorder in sustained remission for more than thirty years, who returns four weeks after his first visit and is seen by ophthalmology on the same day. His eyes have been comfortable, with no pain, redness, discharge, or headache around the eyes. He picked up the naloxone nasal spray; the pharmacy put a braille label on the box and now gives audio labels for any medicine. He received his first-visit summary and results as an accessible electronic document and read them with his screen reader. He has had no opioid or other drug use and no cravings, and he has been touring as usual. He has had no falls. [S]
