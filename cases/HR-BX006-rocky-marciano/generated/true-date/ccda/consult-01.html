Assessment

  • Disc herniation: imaging and surgery (M51.16). An improving left L5 radiculopathy with no motor deficit and no red flags. Most such herniations improve within six to twelve weeks without surgery, and repeat imaging would not change the plan. A surgical opinion would be needed for any new weakness, saddle numbness or bladder or bowel change (the same day), or for disabling leg pain that has not improved after six to twelve weeks of good conservative care. [S]
  • Exercise therapy and return to training (M54.59). Exercise therapy is the main treatment for both the radiculopathy and the chronic back pain: directional preference (extension) exercise, which centralizes his pain today, then motor control and progressive trunk and hip strengthening, twice a week for six weeks, then a home program. Naproxen with food is reasonable for a short course but should be needed less as therapy works; if he needs it most days he should say so rather than keep taking it. No opioids or muscle relaxants.
