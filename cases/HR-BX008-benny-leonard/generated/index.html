Teaching objective: Hypertensive heart disease and sudden cardiac death risk in a man whose job is physical: a 50-year-old professional boxing referee and boxing instructor, a former champion who lost his savings years ago and works to rebuild them, is found at his license examination to have stage 2 hypertension, with breathlessness and chest tightness late in long nights in hot arenas and left ventricular hypertrophy on the ECG. The episode covers the evaluation of exertional symptoms with exercise stress echocardiography under the 2021 AHA/ACC chest pain guideline; telling hypertensive hypertrophy from hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and athlete's heart; sudden cardiac death risk in left ventricular hypertrophy; a coronary calcium score that changes the statin decision under the 2026 ACC/AHA dyslipidemia guideline and the 2023 chronic coronary disease guideline; a two-drug single-pill start, home blood pressure monitoring, laboratory checks after each change and a target below 130/80 mmHg under the 2025 AHA/ACC blood pressure guideline; volume depletion on a thiazide in summer heat, sick-day rules and a change to a calcium channel blocker; aspirin by shared decision; a return-to-work plan for refereeing, with heat, hydration, warning symptoms and defibrillators at the venue; and financial strain and worry as social factors in care, with low-cost generic once-daily medicines.
