## Assessment and plan

1. **Acute posterior vitreous detachment, right eye** (H43.811). Flashes and a new floater confined to one eye, brought on by eye movement and without headache, are typical of a posterior vitreous detachment and not of migraine aura, which affects the vision of both eyes, spreads over minutes and usually precedes a headache. High myopia raises the risk of a retinal tear in the weeks after a detachment, so the eye was examined the same day: no break or detachment. **S**
