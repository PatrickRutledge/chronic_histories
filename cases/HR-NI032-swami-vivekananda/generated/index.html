Teaching objective: Diagnose and classify diabetes in a 35-year-old South Asian man at a near-normal BMI (ADA screens Asian Americans from a BMI of 23 because South Asians develop type 2 diabetes at lower weights and younger ages): confirm with A1c and fasting glucose, test GAD antibodies and C-peptide to exclude type 1 and slowly progressive autoimmune diabetes, and record the decision. Then treat by shared decision with metformin and, once moderately increased albuminuria is confirmed, an SGLT2 inhibitor; build a mostly vegetarian, culturally fitting nutrition plan with a dietitian around his monastic diet; plan safe religious fasting with medication adjustment (IDF-DAR guidance, adapted from Ramadan to Hindu fasts); screen and protect the kidneys (UACR, eGFR, blood pressure with an ARB), eyes and lipids; treat long-standing insomnia without hypnotics; and diagnose and treat adult-onset asthma on GINA track 1, managing the steroid-induced hyperglycemia of an exacerbation. His diet, fasting and monastic practice are his to decide; the clinic adapts to them.

  • S Daily routine: rises before dawn for meditation and worship; teaching and correspondence through the day; often works late into the night. Travels often, in India and abroad, and is now planning a long journey in the mountains from the spring
  • S Diet: eats with the monastery, mostly vegetarian (rice, chapati, dal, vegetables, milk and yogurt, and sweets offered after worship), with fish at some meals by his own choice; keeps religious fasts on the eleventh day of each lunar fortnight and at major festivals, sometimes without water.
