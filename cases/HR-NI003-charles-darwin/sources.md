- **Cause of his illness.** The episodic vomiting, the prodromal trembling, palpitations, and swimming head, the flatulence, and the long course are documented (**D**). The cause is not known. Cyclic vomiting syndrome (certainty possible), Chagas disease (disputed), lactose intolerance (disputed), panic disorder with agoraphobia (possible, marked sensitive), and the other proposed explanations (disputed, marked sensitive because they include psychosomatic accounts) are graded **R** and none is coded.
