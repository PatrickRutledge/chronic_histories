Assessment and Plan

  • Common cold (J00). Viral upper respiratory infection with a clear chest and normal oxygen saturation. Cough is present and there is no tonsillar exudate, so streptococcal pharyngitis is unlikely and no throat test or antibiotic is needed. [S]
    • Honey in warm drinks, fluids, saline nasal spray, and rest.
    • Acetaminophen 1 g up to every 6 hours as needed for fever or sore throat, no more than 3 g a day.
    • Avoid nighttime cold and sleep products containing diphenhydramine, doxylamine, or chlorpheniramine, which add drowsiness, dry mouth, and constipation to amitriptyline.
    • Avoid cough products containing dextromethorphan while on amitriptyline and sumatriptan, and avoid oral decongestants such as pseudoephedrine, which can raise the pulse.

  • Cyclic vomiting syndrome, provisional (R11.15). No prodrome so far. Infection, fever, and lost sleep are recognized triggers, so the abortive plan should be at hand for the next few days. [S]
    • Continue amitriptyline 75 mg at bedtime.
    • At the first sign of the prodrome, take sumatriptan 20 mg nasal spray and ondansetron 8 mg dissolving tablet together, lie in a dark quiet room, and sip fluids.
    • Keep regular small meals and as much sleep as the cough allows.
    • Use the emergency department letter if vomiting continues for more than six hours despite the abortive medicines, or if he cannot keep fluids down.
