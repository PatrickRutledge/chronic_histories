Chief Complaint

Three-month review of cyclic vomiting syndrome on amitriptyline. [S]

History of Present Illness

Mr. Darwin returns eight weeks after starting amitriptyline. The dose was raised to 50 mg after two weeks and to 75 mg four weeks ago, by portal message. He had one episode two weeks into treatment; he took the sumatriptan spray and an ondansetron tablet within half an hour of the prodrome, and it settled in about ten hours without emergency care. He has had no episode in the four weeks since reaching 75 mg, and his cold three weeks ago passed without one. He has a dry mouth, mild constipation, and some grogginess in the first hour of the morning, and has gained 1 kg. He has no dizziness on standing, palpitations outside the prodrome, or urinary hesitancy. He practices the relaxation breathing most evenings and has not yet started cognitive behavioral therapy. No ECG was done at the 50 mg step, because the increase was made by portal message. [S]
