History of Present Illness

Mr. Darwin is a 40-year-old man evaluated six weeks ago for more than a year of episodes of nausea and repeated vomiting, who returns for his results and is seen by gastroenterology on the same day. His diary records three episodes since the first visit, each starting in the early morning or after a social evening, with half an hour to an hour of nausea, sweating, trembling hands, a racing heart, and a swimming head, then vomiting many times over 18 to 30 hours. He lay in a dark room and sipped water, and did not need emergency care. Between episodes he has wind and tires by the afternoon but does not vomit. His weight is steady. He confirmed again that he has never used cannabis and does not take long hot baths or showers during episodes. The blood and urine tests and ECG at the first visit were normal, the CT enterography two weeks later was normal, and the upper endoscopy two weeks ago was normal, with normal duodenal biopsies. [S]
