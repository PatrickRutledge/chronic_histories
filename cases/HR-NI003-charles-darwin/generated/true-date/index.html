Teaching objective: Evaluation and first six months of care of recurrent stereotyped vomiting episodes in a 40-year-old man: a symptom diary, the Rome IV criteria for cyclic vomiting syndrome and its red flags, a cannabis and substance history (to exclude cannabinoid hyperemesis syndrome), a work-up between episodes to exclude other causes (blood count, metabolic and liver panels, lipase, urinalysis, TSH, morning cortisol, celiac serology, CT enterography, and upper endoscopy), a baseline ECG, and anxiety and mood screens (GAD-7, PHQ-9). Management follows the 1842 ANMS and Cyclic Vomiting Syndrome Association guideline: amitriptyline prophylaxis titrated to about 1 mg/kg with ECG checks of the QTc, an abortive plan of nasal sumatriptan and ondansetron at the prodrome, an individualized emergency department protocol, trigger management, and relaxation training. The diagnosis stays provisional because it is clinical, and none of the historical theories of his illness is treated as confirmed.
