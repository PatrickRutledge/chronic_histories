Please advise whether this is cyclic vomiting syndrome, whether the work-up is complete, and on prophylaxis, abortive treatment, and an emergency plan. [S]

  • Charles Darwin was born on February 12, 1809, in Shrewsbury, Shropshire, England. [D]
  • While waiting at Plymouth to sail on the Beagle in late 1831, Darwin was troubled with palpitations and pain about the heart, and consulted no doctor for fear of being judged unfit for the voyage. [D]

  • Darwin first mentioned periodical vomiting in a letter to W. D. Fox in June 1840. [D]

  • Over his adult life he had gastrointestinal distress, including vomiting, retching, and bowel irregularity, with headaches, fatigue, trembling, faintness, dizziness, and skin problems including eczema. [D]
