History of Present Illness

Mr. Darwin returns after three months at amitriptyline 75 mg at bedtime. He has had one episode in that time, three weeks ago, after an overnight journey with little sleep. He used the sumatriptan spray and an ondansetron tablet at the first nausea and trembling, lay down in a dark room, and was well again within about four hours, without vomiting more than twice. He has had no emergency visits. The dry mouth persists, the constipation is better with more fluid and fiber, and the morning grogginess has faded. He walks most days, practices the relaxation breathing most evenings, and has accepted more invitations than in the spring. He still has some wind between episodes, which troubles him less. [S]
