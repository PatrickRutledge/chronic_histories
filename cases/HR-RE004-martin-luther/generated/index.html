Teaching objective: Recognizing and managing Ménière disease in a 43-year-old man with attacks of spinning vertigo, roaring tinnitus and muffled hearing in one ear, under the AAO-HNS 2020 clinical practice guideline and the Bárány Society criteria: a careful history of attack duration and ear symptoms, audiometry to document low-frequency sensorineural hearing loss, MRI to exclude a vestibular schwannoma, vestibular migraine and stroke in the differential, salt and caffeine advice, a diuretic as maintenance, the weak evidence for betahistine, limited use of vestibular suppressants, vestibular rehabilitation for chronic imbalance, and intratympanic steroids held in reserve; recurrent depression recognized alongside a frightening chronic illness, with a DSM-5-TR diagnosis, the PHQ-9, a structured safety assessment, CBT and an SSRI by shared decision under the APA 2019 guideline, and the sodium risk of an SSRI with a thiazide; an acute ureteral stone in a man with an earlier stone, with medical expulsive therapy, stone analysis and a 24-hour urine to guide prevention under the AUA guidelines; and preventive care at 43.
