Teaching objective: Manage hand osteoarthritis in an 85-year-old sculptor who still works with hammer and chisel, by the 2019 ACR/Arthritis Foundation guideline and the CMID osteoarthritis schedule: confirm the diagnosis clinically and exclude inflammatory, crystal, and iron-overload arthritis when the metacarpophalangeal joints are involved; put exercise, hand therapy, joint protection, and a thumb-base splint first; use topical rather than oral NSAIDs, stopping self-directed ibuprofen because of age, kidney function, and blood pressure; offer a thumb-base glucocorticoid injection; measure pain, function, and grip at each visit; keep him working; and keep well-controlled gout and a past stone history quiet while assessing falls.
