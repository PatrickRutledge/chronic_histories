History of Present Illness

Mr. Buonarroti is seen at the request of Dr. Castellano for hand pain. He has carved stone all his working life and still works most mornings. Over about two years the base of his left thumb has become painful and stiff, with knobby swelling of the thumb and index finger and, more recently, the right index and middle fingertips. Morning stiffness lasts about 15 minutes. He has had no hot, swollen hand joints, no rash, psoriasis, or nail change, and no other joint symptoms apart from his past gout, which has been quiet on allopurinol. After four weeks off ibuprofen and on diclofenac gel and a splint, his pain is about 5 of 10. [S]

Assessment
