## Assessment and plan

1. **Orthostatic hypotension from volume depletion** (I95.1). A fall of 24 mmHg on standing, a dry mouth, low home weight and a creatinine of 1.10 mg/dL (from 0.98) fit volume depletion from heat, less fluid and three medicines that remove salt and water. The ECG shows sinus rhythm without arrhythmia, and the neurological examination is normal, with nothing to suggest a stroke or transient ischemic attack. Potassium is 4.4 mmol/L, the first check since spironolactone was added six weeks ago. **S**
   - Sick-day rules: hold chlorthalidone, spironolactone and dapagliflozin for two days; keep taking amlodipine.
   - Drink water through the day, about 2 liters, with one glass of an oral rehydration drink; eat regular meals.
   - Garden in the early morning, not the afternoon; spend the hottest hours in a cooling place such as the library; use fans and cool showers.
   - Restart all three medicines when the lightheadedness has gone and her home weight is back to usual, and keep weighing daily.
   - Stand up slowly; sit at once if lightheaded.
2. **Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction** (I50.32). Dry, not congested: no edema and a low jugular venous pressure. **S**
   - Restart the medicines as above; call if her weight rises more than 1 kg in a day or 2 kg in a week after restarting.
