## Recommendations

1. Treat the knees as the cause of the slower gait: exercise first, diclofenac 1% gel to each painful knee up to four times a day, and acetaminophen as needed within 3,000 mg a day; no X-ray unless the picture changes (question 1).
2. Keep naproxen and diphenhydramine stopped; replace the sleep aid with a fixed sleep routine and no sleep medicine; check every over-the-counter purchase with the clinic or pharmacist (question 2).
3. Twelve weeks of physical therapy with a progressive strength and balance program based on Otago, then tai chi twice a week; repeat gait speed, Timed Up and Go, chair stands, balance and grip after the program (question 3).
4. Falls prevention under STEADI: a home safety check by the physical therapist, a second stair rail and night lights, shoes with a back indoors, and a yearly eye examination; continue vitamin D3 (question 4).
5. DXA of the spine and hips with FRAX; treat with an osteoporosis medicine only if FRAX reaches the BHOF thresholds or there is osteoporosis or a fragility fracture; vitamin D3 1,000 units daily with a recheck after about three months, and calcium from food (question 5).
6. Keep the plan anchored on what matters most to him (4Ms), and offer advance care planning at his wellness visit.
