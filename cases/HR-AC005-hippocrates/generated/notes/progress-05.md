## Advance care planning

Voluntary advance care planning discussion of about 30 minutes, with his consent. He named a health care agent, a long-time friend, and completed an advance directive; the agent is recorded in the chart and is not named here. He said that what matters most to him is to think clearly, to teach, and to stay in his own house. If he became seriously ill with a good chance of recovery he wants full treatment, including a short stay in intensive care; if he could no longer recognize people or speak with them, he would want comfort-focused care at home and no resuscitation, mechanical ventilation or tube feeding. He asked to discuss a portable medical order at a later visit. **S**
