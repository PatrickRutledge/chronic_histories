Teaching objective: Healthy aging in a nonagenarian, with care built on function rather than disease: an 89-year-old physician who still teaches comes in after a wellness screening notes slower walking and a near-fall. The episode covers the comprehensive geriatric assessment (gait speed, Timed Up and Go, chair stands and the four-stage balance test, grip strength, MoCA and Mini-Cog, GDS-15, hearing and vision, nutrition and continence) and frailty (Clinical Frailty Scale and the FRAIL scale, ICFSR 2019); the Age-Friendly Health Systems 4Ms (What Matters, Medication, Mentation, Mobility); a deprescribing review with the AGS Beers Criteria 2023 (over-the-counter naproxen and diphenhydramine stopped); knee osteoarthritis diagnosed clinically without imaging and treated with exercise, topical diclofenac and acetaminophen (ACR/AF 2019); falls prevention by STEADI and the World Falls Guidelines, with a strength and balance program; bone health in an older man (DXA, FRAX and vitamin D); a sick visit for a cold that tests the deprescribing plan; vaccinations for his age (pneumococcal, RSV, recombinant zoster, high-dose influenza); and advance care planning centered on what matters most to him.
