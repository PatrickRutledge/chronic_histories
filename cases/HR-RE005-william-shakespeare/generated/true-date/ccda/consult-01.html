History of Present Illness

Mr. Shakespeare is seen at the request of Dr. Achterberg-Sule, two weeks after his first visit. Assessment

  • Essential tremor (G25.0). An isolated tremor syndrome of bilateral upper-limb action tremor present for at least three years, without tremor elsewhere or other neurological signs: essential tremor by the MDS 1608 criteria. Thyroid and metabolic causes are excluded, and he takes no tremor-inducing drug. Alcohol rebound and caffeine add an enhanced physiological component, and the brief response to alcohol is typical of essential tremor. There are no features of Parkinson disease (no rest or re-emergent tremor, bradykinesia, or rigidity) or of dystonic tremor, so no imaging is needed. [S]
  • Hazardous drinking (Z72.89). Alcohol steadies essential tremor for an hour or so and worsens it the next morning; using it as treatment risks escalating use, so it should be discussed openly as part of the tremor plan. [S]

Plan of Treatment

  • Propranolol extended-release 60 mg each morning, increasing after four weeks to 80 mg if the response is partial and the pulse allows, and up to 160 mg a day if needed; check pulse and blood pressure at each visit; do not stop it suddenly. [S]
  • If propranolol is not tolerated or does not help, primidone starting at 25 mg at night and increased slowly; not both at once for now. [S]
  • No brain MRI or dopamine transporter scan: the diagnosis is clinical and there are no atypical features. [S]
  • Occupational therapy for writing: weighted and wide-grip pens, forearm support, slower and larger script, and dictation for long drafts. [S]
  • Keep drinking within the agreed limits and do not use alcohol to settle the tremor; keep coffee to two cups a day, and avoid decongestants and other stimulants. [S]
  • TETRAS rating with a handwriting sample and spiral at each treatment review; ask me to see him again if a rest tremor, slowness, imbalance, or head or voice tremor appears, or if two first-line drugs fail, when surgical options would be discussed. [S]
