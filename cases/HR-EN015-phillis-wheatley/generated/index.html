Teaching objective: Confirm asthma in a 19-year-old Black woman, born in West Africa, who has been called frail and asthmatic since childhood but never tested: spirometry with bronchodilator response read with race-neutral equations, FeNO and blood eosinophils, with tuberculosis excluded. Then treat on GINA 2025 track 1 (as-needed low-dose budesonide-formoterol, stepping to maintenance-and-reliever therapy after an exacerbation), write an action plan, address her triggers (cold air, cooking and fireplace smoke, colds), prepare her for a long sea journey and a summer away from her clinic, vaccinate, screen for depression and anxiety, and counsel on contraception and pregnancy planning with asthma (budesonide preferred, controller continued in pregnancy). Her social history at the true-frame index date is enslavement: the modern episode renders it truthfully as domestic servitude, and the clinic practises trauma-informed, patient-centred care in which she alone consents, decides and receives her information.
