Teaching objective: Provide disability-competent primary and preventive care to a 78-year-old writer and advocate who has been deafblind since infancy: communicate with her directly through a qualified tactile interpreter and braille materials on her terms, with the time that takes; evaluate a fall by CDC STEADI and prevent the next one with strength and balance exercise and home changes; screen bone density; adapt cognitive and mood screening honestly; protect the sense of touch she relies on; and give the same screening and prevention any woman her age would get.

Social, functional, and mental status

Social history

  • S Occupation: author and advocate for people who are blind or deafblind; still writes daily, answers a large correspondence, and travels several times a year to speak for a national foundation for blind people
  • S Communication: receives language by tactile fingerspelling with the one-hand manual alphabet, her hand resting lightly on the speller's hand; speaks for herself; reads and writes braille and uses a refreshable braille display for email; reads the lips of familiar people by touch
  • S Never smoker; a glass of wine with dinner a few times a week; no other substance use; walks outdoors daily with a sighted guide
  • S Lives in her own house in the country with a long-time live-in assistant and a dog
  • S Supports: her assistant, a wide circle of friends and colleagues, and the deafblind community

Functional status

  • S Independent in all activities of daily living; her assistant helps with driving, shopping, and print mail; moves through her home by touch along walls and rails, and outdoors with a sighted guide
  • S At intake: Timed Up and Go 13.5 seconds, 30-second chair stand 9, and tandem stance held 6 seconds, below the norms for her age
  • S By the six-month review: Timed Up and Go 11.2 seconds, chair stand 12, tandem stance 10 seconds; no further falls

Mental status

  • S Alert, warm, witty, and precise; speech clear to those who know her and understandable with attention; follows rapid fingerspelling easily
  • S Mood good; PHQ-9 score 2 completed in braille; cognition intact on a tactile interview (see the consultation)
