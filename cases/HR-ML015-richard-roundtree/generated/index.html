Teaching objective: Men get breast cancer. Evaluate a new breast lump in a man of 51 promptly, without accepting an earlier history of gynecomastia as an explanation: examination that tells a firm, eccentric cancer from soft, central gynecomastia, same-week diagnostic mammography and ultrasound of both breasts and both axillae, and core biopsy of the mass and any abnormal node; ER, PR, and HER2 status; germline genetic testing and counseling for every man with breast cancer; surgical planning with a breast surgical oncologist; lymphedema prevention; and preparation for chemotherapy followed by tamoxifen, the preferred endocrine therapy for men, on the ASCO male breast cancer guideline, the NCCN breast guideline, and the CMID breast cancer monograph. His own public account of embarrassment and of keeping his treatment secret frames the teaching on the stigma that delays men's care.

Procedures and immunizations

Procedures

  • S Diagnostic mammography of both breasts: 1.6 cm irregular, spiculated retroareolar mass eccentric to the left nipple (BI-RADS 5); right breast with no mass and no gynecomastia (BI-RADS 1)
  • S Ultrasound of both breasts and both axillae: 1.5 cm irregular hypoechoic mass with angular margins next to the left nipple; one left axillary lymph node with a thickened cortex; right breast and right axilla normal
  • S Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the left breast mass and the left axillary node: invasive ductal carcinoma, grade 3; estrogen receptor 95 percent, progesterone receptor 85 percent, HER2 1+ (negative), Ki-67 30 percent; the node contains metastatic carcinoma
  • S Mastectomy with level I and II axillary lymph node dissection for left breast cancer. Pathology of the left breast: invasive ductal carcinoma 1.6 cm, grade 3, margins clear, no lymphovascular invasion; 2 of 15 lymph nodes involved (largest 7 mm), no extranodal extension; pT1c pN1a, stage IIA
  • S Transthoracic echocardiogram before anthracycline chemotherapy: left ventricular ejection fraction 62 percent; normal valves and wall motion
