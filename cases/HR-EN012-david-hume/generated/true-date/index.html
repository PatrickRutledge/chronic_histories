Procedures and immunizations

Procedures

  • S Colonoscopy to the cecum with biopsy: 4 cm ulcerated, partly circumferential, non-obstructing mass at 28 cm in the sigmoid colon, tattooed; no other lesions. Biopsy: moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma, mismatch-repair proteins (MLH1, PMS2, MSH2, MSH6) intact
  • S CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast: sigmoid wall thickening with two pericolic lymph nodes up to 7 mm; lungs clear; one 9 mm indeterminate low-density lesion in liver segment VI
  • S MRI of the liver with contrast: the 9 mm segment VI lesion is a typical hemangioma, with peripheral nodular enhancement and progressive fill-in; no liver metastases
  • S Laparoscopic sigmoid colectomy with primary anastomosis and no stoma. Pathology: adenocarcinoma, moderately differentiated, pT3 N1a (1 of 22 nodes), margins clear, no lymphovascular or perineural invasion; stage IIIB
  • S Adjuvant CAPOX chemotherapy, four 21-day cycles (three months), given by medical oncology
