Teaching objective: Evaluate bone health in a 63-year-old postmenopausal woman after a fall with a hand fracture: read the DXA T-scores precisely (low bone mass, not osteoporosis by T-score), calculate FRAX with her risk factors, and base the treatment decision on the guideline thresholds; complete a secondary-causes work-up and correct vitamin D before an antiresorptive; choose therapy strictly by guideline and her risk; add calcium, vitamin D, weight-bearing and balance exercise, and falls prevention; set the DXA follow-up interval and the drug-holiday reassessment; and give recovery-oriented care for an alcohol use disorder in long remission, with a structured safety assessment, on the CMID osteoporosis schedule.
