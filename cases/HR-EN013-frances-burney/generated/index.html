Teaching objective: Evaluate a palpable breast mass in a postmenopausal woman of 59 with diagnostic mammography, ultrasound, and core biopsy; complete receptor testing, extent-of-disease MRI, and germline testing; coordinate mastectomy with sentinel node biopsy under modern general and regional anesthesia; recognize and manage a postoperative seroma; and begin an aromatase inhibitor with the bone-health, lipid, lymphedema, and distress monitoring of the CMID breast cancer protocol.

Procedures and immunizations

Procedures

  • S Diagnostic bilateral mammography: 2.4 cm irregular spiculated mass at 10 o'clock in the upper outer right breast; heterogeneously dense breasts; left breast normal; BI-RADS 5
  • S Right breast and axillary ultrasound: 2.5 cm irregular hypoechoic mass at 10 o'clock, 6 cm from the nipple; normal axillary lymph nodes
  • S Ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the right breast mass with clip placement: invasive ductal carcinoma, grade 2; ER 95 percent, PR 70 percent, HER2 1+ (negative), Ki-67 15 percent
  • S Bilateral breast MRI with contrast for extent of disease: index mass 2.6 cm; a second 0.8 cm enhancing mass at 7 o'clock in the right breast, 5 cm from the index mass; left breast normal; no enlarged nodes
  • S MRI-guided core needle biopsy of the second right breast lesion: invasive ductal carcinoma, grade 2, ER positive, HER2 negative (multicentric disease)
  • S Right total mastectomy without reconstruction, her choice, under general anesthesia; overnight stay. Pathology: multicentric invasive ductal carcinoma, 2.6 cm and 0.8 cm, grade 2, margins clear, no lymphovascular invasion; pT2(m) pN0(sn), anatomic stage IIA. Recurrence score (21-gene assay) 13
  • S Right axillary sentinel lymph node biopsy at mastectomy (radioisotope and blue dye): 0 of 3 nodes involved
  • S Ultrasound-guided pectoral nerve block (PECS II) with ropivacaine before incision, for pain control after mastectomy
  • S Baseline bone density scan (DXA) before anastrozole: lumbar spine T-score -1.4, femoral neck T-score -1.7 (low bone mass)
  • S Aspiration of a right chest wall seroma by the breast surgery team: 140 mL of clear straw-colored fluid; no signs of infection
