Teaching objective: Severe alcohol use disorder in a woman living in rural poverty: a 44-year-old who does odd jobs and moves between towns comes to a clinic a few weeks after a stay on a county poor farm. The episode covers a nonjudgmental, trauma-informed assessment with the AUDIT, DSM-5-TR criteria and PEth, withdrawal risk with the PAWSS and CIWA-Ar, outpatient withdrawal management with daily checks under the ASAM guideline, parenteral thiamine, electrolytes and nutrition, naltrexone under the APA guideline and the change to a monthly injection when daily pills are hard to keep, liver assessment with FIB-4 and transient elastography, depressive symptoms judged after weeks without alcohol, a structured safety assessment with firearm storage and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, peer recovery support and mutual-help choices, harm reduction and a return to drinking met without blame, the ways alcohol harms women more, and help with housing, food, income and health coverage through real public programs.
