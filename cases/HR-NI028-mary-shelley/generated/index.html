Teaching objective: Evaluate headaches with transient numbness and weakness of one side in a 51-year-old writer: distinguish focal seizures from transient ischemic attack and migraine aura, obtain MRI with gadolinium and an EEG, and give driving and seizure-safety advice at once. Confirm a left convexity meningioma over the motor cortex with vasogenic edema and focal epilepsy; start levetiracetam and monitor mood and irritability; use dexamethasone only for symptomatic edema and briefly; present observation, resection, and stereotactic radiosurgery by shared decision-making (EANO 2021 and NCCN); grade the tumor by the WHO CNS5 criteria after resection, decide on adjuvant treatment by grade and extent of resection, and set the surveillance MRI schedule. Throughout, take a grief history and screen for depression in a woman with many early losses, and protect her ability to write. The teaching note contrasts this with the supposed tumour of the left hemisphere on her death certificate and the hemiplegic migraine hypothesis.
