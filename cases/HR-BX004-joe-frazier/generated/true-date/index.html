Teaching objective: Type 2 diabetes and hypertension in a 64-year-old Black man, a former professional heavyweight boxer who runs a gym, managed under the ADA Standards of Care 2008, the 2007 AHA/ACC blood pressure guideline and the CMID monographs: A1c, kidney function by the race-free CKD-EPI 2003 equation and urine albumin, lipids and a high-intensity statin, a GLP-1 receptor agonist with cardiovascular and kidney benefit, blood pressure to below 130/80 with home readings, eye and foot examinations, and cost and access. The yearly liver check finds a high FIB-4, and the AASLD 2005 MASLD guidance leads to elastography and a diagnosis of compensated cirrhosis from MASLD; under the AASLD 2005 hepatocellular carcinoma guidance he begins ultrasound with AFP every six months, the kind of surveillance that can find liver cancer early, with variceal screening, vaccines and no alcohol. Brain health in a former boxer is addressed with a documented cognitive screen and risk-factor care, and without a diagnosis of chronic traumatic encephalopathy, which can be made only after death.
