  • S Advance care planning conversation, about 15 minutes
  • S Left frontal craniotomy with neuronavigation for complete excision of the orbitofrontal cavernous malformation with the surrounding hemosiderin-stained brain, sparing the developmental venous anomaly; no complications; home on the third day. Pathology: cavernous malformation (closely packed, thin-walled vascular channels without intervening brain, with old hemorrhage and hemosiderin in the surrounding gliotic tissue)
  • S MRI of the brain the day after surgery: complete resection of the cavernous malformation and the hemosiderin rim; expected postoperative changes; developmental venous anomaly preserved; no hemorrhage or infarct
