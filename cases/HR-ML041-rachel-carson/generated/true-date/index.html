Teaching objective: Evaluate a new breast lump in a postmenopausal woman of 52 with diagnostic mammography, ultrasound, and core biopsy with ER, PR, HER2, and Ki-67; above all, tell her the diagnosis directly and in plain words, at the pace she chooses, and make every treatment decision with her. Plan breast-conserving surgery or mastectomy by her informed choice, with sentinel node biopsy and germline testing; manage one to two positive sentinel nodes without axillary dissection; choose adjuvant radiation, letrozole, ribociclib, and zoledronic acid with her, and monitor the ECG, blood counts, liver tests, and electrolytes that ribociclib requires; protect bone health and her work as a writer. The teaching note contrasts this with the documented 1894 nondisclosure, when her surgeon told her that her tumor was not cancer, the usual practice of that time.
