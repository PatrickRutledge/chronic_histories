Teaching objective: Primary care for a 35-year-old professional baseball player with a year of progressive weakness and loss of coordination: recognizing mixed upper and lower motor neuron signs, excluding mimics (MRI of the brain and cervical spine, blood tests, EMG and nerve conduction studies), and making the diagnosis of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis on the Gold Coast and revised El Escorial criteria; riluzole with liver test monitoring, genetic testing (tofersen only for an SOD1 variant) and edaravone discussed; a multidisciplinary ALS clinic tracking ALSFRS-R, forced vital capacity and weight; nutrition, speech and swallowing, mobility equipment, noninvasive ventilation thresholds, work accommodation, mood screening, and early advance care planning, on the AAN ALS practice parameters (2009).
