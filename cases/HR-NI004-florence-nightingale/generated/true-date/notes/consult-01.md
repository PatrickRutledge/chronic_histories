## Assessment

1. **Personal history of treated brucellosis** (Z86.19). No evidence of relapse or a focal complication. Relapses usually come within months of treatment and present with fever, which she has not had; she has no spinal or sacroiliac findings, no murmur, no organ enlargement, and normal inflammatory markers and blood count. **S**
2. **Repeat testing**. Brucella agglutinating antibodies can stay positive for a long time after successful treatment, so a repeat titer cannot separate past from active infection and would only cause confusion. Blood cultures are useful only if fever returns. **S**
3. **Other infections**. Nothing in her exposures calls for further infection testing now. The one-time HIV, hepatitis B, and hepatitis C screening recommended for all adults is reasonable to complete with her preventive care. **S**
4. **Post-infectious ME/CFS** (G93.32). Her course fits a post-infectious fatigue syndrome after brucellosis rather than ongoing infection. Prolonged or repeated antibiotics do not help post-infectious fatigue and carry real harms. **S**
