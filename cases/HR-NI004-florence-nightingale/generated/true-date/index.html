Social, functional, and mental status

Social history

  • S Occupation: director of nursing quality and hospital statistics for a regional health system; previously led nurses on an overseas field-hospital deployment; now works about three hours a day from home, much of it lying on a sofa or in bed
  • S Never smoker; alcohol rarely; no other substance use; simple diet; drinks about 1.2 L of fluid a day
  • S Lives alone in a ground-floor apartment; groceries delivered; a housekeeper helps twice a week
  • S Supports: a small team of trusted colleagues who visit her at home to work, and a wide network of correspondents

Functional status

  • S Moderate ME/CFS at intake: independent in self-care but housebound most days, walks about 10 minutes before symptoms worsen, and needs a rest after showering; works about three hours a day, reclined, in short blocks
  • S By the six-month review: fewer crashes, out of the house twice a week, and working four to five hours a day in planned blocks with rest between

Mental status

  • S Alert, precise, and articulate; tires visibly toward the end of a long conversation and loses words at times
  • S Mood frustrated by her limits but not depressed; no hopelessness; PHQ-9 score 8 drawn mainly from the fatigue, sleep, and concentration items, with item 9 scored 0
