History of Present Illness

Dr. Kowalczyk reviewed the pathology and agreed that he needs no further treatment, and set out the surveillance schedule. Erections have not returned since the operation; he understands why and does not want treatment now. He is back at full-time work, walks 20 to 30 minutes a day, and his appetite is good. [S]
