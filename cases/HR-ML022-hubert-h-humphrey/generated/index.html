Teaching objective: Primary care after radical cystoprostatectomy with an ileal conduit for urothelial bladder cancer, on the AUA/ASCO/SUO muscle-invasive bladder cancer guideline, the AUA/SUO non-muscle-invasive guideline, and the CMID urologic cancer monograph: how high-risk non-muscle-invasive disease that recurs after intravesical therapy and earlier pelvic radiation leads to early cystectomy, and how often the final pathology is upstaged; why pT2a N0 disease needs no adjuvant therapy; surveillance by cross-sectional imaging every 6 to 12 months and upper-tract and kidney function checks every 6 months; the metabolic effects of an ileal conduit (hyperchloremic acidosis, kidney function, vitamin B12); stoma and peristomal skin care; and the difference between expected bacteriuria in conduit urine and a true kidney infection, with a culture taken by catheter from the stoma. His documented history of bladder growths, intravesical thiotepa with X-ray therapy, and a radical cystectomy with a small-intestine conduit frames the episode.
