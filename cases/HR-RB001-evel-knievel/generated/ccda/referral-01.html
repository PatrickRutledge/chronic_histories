Plan of Treatment

Guideline: AASLD-IDSA HCV Guidance: Recommendations for Testing, Managing, and Treating Hepatitis C. Simplified HCV treatment algorithm for treatment-naive adults with compensated cirrhosis (2023); Hepatitis C Virus Infection in Adolescents and Adults: Screening (2020) [S]

Guideline: Rakoski M, Goyal P, Spencer-Safier M, et al. Pain management in patients with cirrhosis. Clin Liver Dis (Hoboken) 2018;11(6):135-140 (PMID 30992804) (2018); Dowell D, et al.
