Teaching objective: Evaluate and treat major depression in a 69-year-old woman who holds a demanding lifelong public office, after several bereavements, following the 2019 APA depression guideline (combined second-generation antidepressant and interpersonal psychotherapy for older adults), the 2022 VA/DoD MDD guideline, the CMID depression monograph, and the 2023 AGS Beers Criteria: a presenting problem of low mood, poor sleep, poor intake, and weight loss worked up for medical causes (blood count, metabolic panel, calcium, albumin, thyroid, vitamin B12, folate, a cognitive screen, and a medication review that stops an anticholinergic sleep aid); PHQ-9 and GDS-15 for measurement-based care; suicide-risk assessment with a passive death wish, a safety plan, and the 988 Lifeline, reviewed at every visit; sertraline started low and raised slowly, with a sodium check for SSRI-related hyponatremia; CBT for insomnia rather than hypnotics; nutrition, dental care, and weight; falls prevention while starting an SSRI; grief distinguished from depression; and the privacy of a public figure.

Social, functional, and mental status

Social history

  • S Occupation: has held a lifelong senior public office as head of state for more than forty years; long days of audiences, papers, and ceremonies; her health is a matter of public interest, and she asks that her care be kept strictly private
  • S Never married; no children; lives in an official residence with long-serving household staff; no firearms in the residence, and her medicines are kept by her own maid
  • S Never smoker; a small glass of wine with dinner on some days; no other substance use; AUDIT-C 1
  • S Bereavement: over the past two years several of the people closest to her have died, including a former protégé and confidant after a bitter breach, whose death she still cannot speak of without distress, and two friends of her own generation; she calls herself the last of her circle
  • S Supports: a few women of her household who have served her for decades, her chaplain, and daily prayer; she walks in the gardens most days

Functional status

  • S Independent in all activities of daily living; still carrying out her duties, but she postpones audiences, reads papers without taking them in, and has given up music and riding

Mental status

  • S Thin, upright, carefully dressed and made up; formal and guarded at first, then candid; speech slow and quiet; frequent sighs; good eye contact
  • S Mood "heavy at heart"; affect constricted and tearful when she speaks of those who have died; thought process linear; a passive wish not to wake up on some mornings, with no intent, plan, or preparatory behavior; no psychotic features; MoCA 27; insight and judgment good
