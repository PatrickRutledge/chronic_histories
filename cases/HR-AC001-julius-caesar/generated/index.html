Teaching objective: Evaluation of recurrent transient episodes of collapse in a 54-year-old man, separating seizure from convulsive syncope and transient ischemic attack (history from a witness, orthostatic vital signs, ECG and ambulatory cardiac monitoring under the ACC/AHA/HRS 2017 syncope guideline; EEG and MRI with diffusion-weighted and epilepsy-protocol sequences; CT angiography of the head and neck and echocardiography), and the diagnosis of late-onset focal epilepsy with a structural vascular cause from a clinically unrecognized cortical infarct, classified by the ILAE 2017 seizure and epilepsy classifications and the ILAE 2014 practical definition. Management follows the AAN/AES 2015 first-seizure guideline (recurrence risk and the case for treatment), the AAN/AES 2018 guideline on newer antiseizure medicines (levetiracetam, with mood monitoring), and the AHA/ASA 2021 secondary stroke prevention guideline with the CMID stroke, hypertension, and hyperlipidemia monographs (aspirin, high-intensity atorvastatin to an LDL below 70 mg/dL, blood pressure below 130/80 mmHg, 30-day rhythm monitoring, depression and cognitive screening), together with seizure safety and driving restrictions, an intercurrent respiratory infection, and an adult preventive visit. The historical dispute between epilepsy and cerebrovascular disease stays unresolved (R); the modern episode makes no claim about what he had.
