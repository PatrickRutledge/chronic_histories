Teaching objective: De novo metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer in a 66-year-old Chilean poet and diplomat posted abroad, on the NCCN prostate cancer guideline, the AUA/SUO advanced prostate cancer guideline and the CMID prostate cancer monograph. Urinary symptoms and bone pain with a hard prostate and a raised PSA lead to MRI, an MRI-targeted biopsy, conventional staging (CT and bone scan) and PSMA PET/CT; the disease is classified as low volume, and the choice is continuous androgen deprivation with apalutamide plus radiotherapy to the prostate, without docetaxel. The episode covers flare protection, early PSA response and the three-month PSA, germline and somatic testing, bone health (DXA, FRAX, vitamin D, calcium, exercise, a dental check and denosumab at the osteoporosis dose), the cardiometabolic risks of androgen deprivation, apalutamide's drug interaction with amlodipine, its rash and its effect on thyroid function, hot flashes, sexual health, fatigue, mood, and care in Spanish, his first language, around his writing and diplomatic work. His documented prostate cancer, operated on in France in July 1971 while he was ambassador, frames the episode; the dispute over his death two years later is history only.
