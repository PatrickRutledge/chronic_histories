Assessment

  • Prostate adenocarcinoma, Grade Group 4, cT3a N1 M1b, de novo metastatic hormone-sensitive disease, low volume (C61). The diagnosis is confirmed. By the conventional-imaging definition used in the trials and the AUA/SUO guideline, high volume means four or more bone metastases with at least one outside the spine and pelvis, or visceral metastases; he has two bone metastases, both within the spine and pelvis, and no visceral disease, so the disease is low volume. The PSMA PET/CT shows nothing more. He has symptoms from the bone metastasis in the right ilium. [S]
  • Systemic treatment (Z79.899). Androgen deprivation should be combined with an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor. Apalutamide, enzalutamide, darolutamide and abiraterone with prednisone are all reasonable. Apalutamide is a single daily tablet without a steroid and improved survival in low-volume disease in the TITAN trial; abiraterone needs prednisone and can worsen blood pressure and glucose, which matters with his hypertension and prediabetes. Docetaxel is not recommended: in low-volume disease it did not clearly improve survival, and triplet therapy is reserved for selected patients with high-volume disease. Apalutamide started a week before the first leuprolide injection also blocks the testosterone flare, so bicalutamide is not needed. [S]
  • Radiotherapy to the prostate. In low-volume metastatic disease, radiotherapy to the prostate added to androgen deprivation improved survival in STAMPEDE, and the guidelines support it in selected patients. It is recommended, about six weeks after starting androgen deprivation, as 55 Gy in 20 daily fractions. [S]
  • Bone health and bone metastases (M85.89). Bone-protective drugs at the monthly doses used to prevent skeletal events are recommended in castration-resistant disease; in hormone-sensitive disease, early zoledronic acid did not reduce skeletal events (CALGB 90202). His own fracture risk is the reason to treat: FRAX with androgen deprivation counted gives a hip fracture risk of 3.4 percent, above the 3 percent threshold. Denosumab 60 mg every six months, the osteoporosis dose, is recommended after a dental examination and once vitamin D replacement has begun and calcium is normal. The painful iliac metastasis is likely to settle on androgen deprivation; radiotherapy to that bone remains an option if it does not. [S]
  • Genetic testing. Germline testing is recommended for every patient with metastatic prostate cancer, and somatic testing of the tumor for metastatic disease; the results could change later treatment (a PARP inhibitor for a BRCA2 alteration) and would matter for his relatives. Pre-test counseling was done today. [S]
  • Drug interactions and hypertension (I10). Apalutamide is a strong CYP3A4 inducer and will lower amlodipine levels, so blood pressure may rise in the first weeks. It also raises the risk of rash, falls, fractures and raised TSH. [S]
