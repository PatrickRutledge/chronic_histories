  • Billie Holiday was born Eleanora Fagan in Philadelphia on April 7, 1915, and was raised in Baltimore by her mother, Sarah Julia "Sadie" Fagan. [D]
  • Her mother died in October 1945; afterwards Holiday drank more heavily and her drug use increased as she grieved. [D]

  • Holiday sought treatment for her addiction, at one point checking herself into an institution in the hope of breaking her habit. [D]
  • Her conviction cost her the New York City cabaret card she needed to perform in clubs that served alcohol. [D]
  • The Federal Bureau of Narcotics pursued Holiday for years, and later writers have described that pursuit as driven by racism and linked it to her performance of "Strange Fruit," a song against lynching. [R]
