  • S Tobacco: former cigarette smoker, about four packs a day for many years and well over 20 pack-years; quit cold turkey about seven years ago and has not smoked since; no e-cigarettes
  • S Alcohol: one or two drinks a week; no other substance use
  • S Exercise: plays golf when the weather allows; walks 30 to 40 minutes most days since cardiac rehabilitation
  • S Married; lives with his spouse; supportive family and close working staff

Functional status

Procedures and immunizations

Procedures

  • S Coronary angiography and percutaneous coronary intervention: occluded proximal left anterior descending artery treated with one drug-eluting stent; no other significant stenosis. Performed at an out-of-state hospital after a late presentation with continuing chest pain
  • S Transthoracic echocardiography in the hospital: ejection fraction 45 percent with anterior and apical hypokinesis, no thrombus
  • S Outpatient cardiac rehabilitation, phase 2: 36 sessions completed; exit exercise test 9 METs without angina, ischemic ECG changes, or arrhythmia
  • S 12-lead electrocardiogram: sinus rhythm 62/min, Q waves in V1 to V3 from the old anterior infarction, no acute ST change
  • S Transthoracic echocardiography: ejection fraction 52 percent, mild apical hypokinesis, no thrombus or aneurysm, normal valves
