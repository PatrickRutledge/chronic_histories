  1. Index age and date shift. The heart attack occurred on September 24, 1955, when Eisenhower was 64, and he was back on a full schedule by early 1956. Chronic-care visits follow on January 26, March 16, May 11, and September 28, 2026 (true frame the same days in 1956).
  2. Modern version of the documented infarction. The anterolateral ST-elevation infarction, the late presentation, and the golf on the day before are D. In the modern episode the same event is treated with a stent, an echocardiogram, and cardiac rehabilitation (all S); the 1955 treatment is not described beyond the documented hospital stay. The synthetic infarction date (September 24, 2025) keeps the true month and day, so it maps to the documented date in the true frame. The hospital ejection fraction of 45 percent, the recovery to 52 percent, the single-vessel disease, the lipid values, and the hypertension are synthetic.
