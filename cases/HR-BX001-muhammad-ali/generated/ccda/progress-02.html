History of Present Illness

Mr. Ali is a 54-year-old man with young-onset Parkinson's disease, diagnosed twelve years ago, who returns four weeks after his first visit and is seen by movement disorders neurology on the same day. Slowness of movement is still his main limitation: buttoning, handwriting, and getting out of low chairs take him longer, and he feels the slowness most in the late morning and late afternoon. The right-hand tremor is mild and settles when he uses the hand. People still have trouble hearing him in a crowd. He takes carbidopa-levodopa 25/100 mg one tablet three times a day, often with meals, and has noticed no wearing-off before doses, no involuntary movements, and no lightheadedness on standing. He has had no falls or near falls. He sleeps well, has no dream enactment, and has no hallucinations or new urges to gamble, shop, or eat. [S]
