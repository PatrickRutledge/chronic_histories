Teaching objective: Long-term care of a man with young-onset Parkinson's disease twelve years after diagnosis: a structured motor examination and Hoehn and Yahr stage at every visit; levodopa dose and timing adjusted for bradykinesia with supine and standing blood pressure checks; screening for falls, swallowing problems, mood, cognition, sleep, autonomic symptoms, psychosis, and impulse control; speech therapy for soft speech and physical therapy for gait and balance; avoidance of dopamine-blocking drugs; and advance care planning, on the CMID Parkinson's disease schedule with AAN and MDS guidance. Functional status

  • S At the first visit, in the on state about two hours after a levodopa dose: Hoehn and Yahr stage 2.5; MDS-UPDRS Part III 30. Bilateral bradykinesia and rigidity, worse on the right; right-hand rest tremor; reduced facial expression and blinking; soft, monotone speech; reduced arm swing with short steps; recovers unaided on the pull test after three steps.
