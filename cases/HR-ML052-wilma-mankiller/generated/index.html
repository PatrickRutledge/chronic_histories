Teaching objective: New generalized myasthenia gravis in a 34-year-old Cherokee woman recovering from major trauma, on the 2016 and 2020 international consensus guidance for MG and the KDIGO 2025 ADPKD guideline. Drooping eyelids, double vision and fatigable weakness lead to the ice pack test, acetylcholine receptor antibodies (with MuSK held in reserve), repetitive nerve stimulation (with single-fiber EMG held in reserve), chest CT for thymoma, TSH, and forced vital capacity and negative inspiratory force, with the warning signs of myasthenic crisis. Treatment covers pyridostigmine, prednisone started low and raised slowly, azathioprine as a steroid-sparing agent after TPMT and infection screening, vaccination before immunosuppression, early thymectomy under the MGTX evidence, the place of newer targeted therapies such as efgartigimod and eculizumab, and the medicines to avoid in myasthenia gravis. Her documented hereditary polycystic kidney disease adds eGFR, urine albumin, kidney ultrasound with total kidney volume and the Mayo Imaging Classification, home blood pressure, water intake and avoiding NSAIDs. Care is culturally safe, with laboratory work through Cherokee Nation Health Services near her home and her own Cherokee medicine respected. Her documented diagnosis of myasthenia gravis less than a year after the 1979 crash, and her recovery with the help of Cherokee medicine people, surgery and drug therapy, frame the episode.

  • S Household: lives with her two daughters, who are not named and are given no role in her care
  • S Tobacco: never. Alcohol: none. No drug use
  • S Health care: eligible for Cherokee Nation Health Services as a citizen; prefers laboratory work at a Cherokee Nation clinic near her home; drives herself again since the winter, about an hour to this clinic
