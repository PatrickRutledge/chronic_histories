  1. Sources. The history rests on the Oklahoma Historical Society encyclopedia, the National Women's History Museum, the Encyclopedia of the Great Plains, the Oklahoma Hall of Fame, Encyclopedia.com, News On 6 (2000), The Daily Beast's summary of the AP obituary, and two Cherokee Phoenix reports (2010). The New York Times obituary (paywalled; a reprint site returned an error) and the Cherokee Nation biography page (its text did not load) were not used. Wikipedia was not used.
  2. Recent-death rule. She died in 2010, so the rule applies. The record uses only what she made public and what obituaries and reference works reported. No sick visit is added. The emotional trauma after the crash (CLM-0006) is marked sensitive and recorded only as published; no mental health diagnosis is made, and the modern mood screens are synthetic and below the threshold for a safety assessment. Nothing in the modern episode adds an illness beyond those documented: blood pressure is kept normal-high without a hypertension diagnosis, and kidney function and urine albumin are normal.
