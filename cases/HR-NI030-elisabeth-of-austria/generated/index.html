Teaching objective: Recognizing and managing a restrictive eating disorder in a 56-year-old woman who presents with weight loss, fatigue, ankle swelling and dizziness, with compulsive exercise and frequent self-weighing since early adulthood: DSM-5-TR anorexia nervosa, restricting type, in later life; medical stabilization under the APA 2023 guideline, the SAHM 2022 medical management paper (written for younger patients and applied with care), the AED medical care standards and the ASPEN refeeding consensus (orthostatic vital signs, ECG for bradycardia and QTc, electrolytes with phosphate and magnesium, glucose, thiamine before nutrition, refeeding risk, and admission thresholds); bone density by DXA; a multidisciplinary team with an eating disorders psychiatrist, a dietitian and CBT-E; recurrent depression with persistent grief, with a structured safety assessment and a safety plan; a gastroenteritis that threatens potassium and the QTc on sertraline; preventive care at 56; the privacy of a public figure; and continuity of care for a patient who travels for weeks at a time.
