History of Present Illness

Empress Elisabeth, 56, is seen at the request of Dr. Marchetti-Osei. Since her late teens she has restricted her eating, with single-food meals, a fixed upper weight of 50 kg that she has kept below for decades, tight lacing, and four to six hours of fast walking a day with gymnastics each morning; she feels anxious and guilty if she cannot exercise. She weighs herself up to three times a day. Over six months she has lost about 6 kg, and she has become tired, cold, dizzy on standing and swollen at the ankles. She describes an intense fear of gaining weight and sees herself as larger than she is, though she accepts that she is too thin. She denies binge eating, vomiting, laxatives, diuretics or diet pills. Her mood has been low for years, worse since the death of her son five years ago, a bereavement by suicide; she has passive thoughts of being better off dead without intent, plan or past attempts. She has had a week of the meal plan and thiamine, missed the blood test planned for three to four days, and has walked about two hours a day. She travels constantly and fears that her illness will become public. [S]

  • Through her life she followed many diets; for periods each meal was a single food, such as egg, a milk product, orange or broth, and her 'magic weight' was fifty kilos.   • Her waist measured only about 50 cm at its smallest, in tightly laced corsets and dresses sewn around it. [D]

She was one of the best horsewomen in Europe, but gave up riding at the beginning of the 1880s because of health problems. [D]

  • It is said that her son's death prompted her to wear only mourning from then on. She suffered from ever-deepening depressions, which in her last years are thought to have developed into a kind of death-wish. [D]
  • After her son's death she sought to master her inner unrest with extended travels and a retreat to Corfu, where she had the Achilleion, a palatial villa, built; she lost interest in it once it was finished and left the island six years after its completion. She often travelled incognito. [D]

Assessment

  • Anorexia nervosa, restricting type, moderate (DSM-5-TR) (F50.011). Restriction leading to a significantly low weight, intense fear of weight gain with persistent behavior that prevents it, and a disturbed experience of her body meet the DSM-5-TR criteria for anorexia nervosa; with no binge eating or purging in the last three months it is the restricting type, and a BMI of 16.0 at intake is moderate. OSFED does not apply because all criteria are met. Most anorexia nervosa in later life, as here, began in youth and became long-standing. The normal TSH, CRP, celiac antibody and electrolytes, with low rather than high glucose, make thyroid disease, inflammation, celiac disease, diabetes and adrenal insufficiency unlikely; no further tests are needed. Compulsive exercise is part of the disorder. [S]
  • Level of care and refeeding. She does not meet the thresholds for admission: her pulse is above 40 awake, systolic pressure above 90, temperature above 35.6 C, potassium above 3.0 mmol/L, and she is eating. Admission would be advised for any of those, for fainting, chest pain or arrhythmia, phosphate below 2.0 mg/dL, a fall in weight despite treatment, or food refusal. Her phosphate of 2.3 mg/dL is an early refeeding effect that needs replacement and weekly checks. The slightly worse ankle edema is refeeding edema; a diuretic would worsen it later. [S]
  • Recurrent depression with persistent grief (F33.1). A recurrent moderate depressive episode with persistent grief, bereavement by suicide, and passive thoughts of death without intent or plan. Antidepressants work poorly at very low weight and starvation mimics depression, so nutrition comes first; an SSRI with low QT risk, such as sertraline, is reasonable once nutrition is under way and the ECG is repeated. [S]
  • Bones and exercise. Decades of low weight and her age put her at high risk of low bone mass; DXA is indicated. Weight restoration is the main treatment. Exercise should be limited while her pulse and blood pressure are abnormal. [S]
