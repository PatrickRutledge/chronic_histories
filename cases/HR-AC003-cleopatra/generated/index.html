Teaching objective: Evaluation of a uterus large for dates in early pregnancy (dating by the last period checked against the crown-rump length under ACOG Committee Opinion 700; multiple pregnancy, dating error, fibroids, and molar pregnancy) and high-risk prenatal care of a dichorionic diamniotic twin pregnancy in a 29-year-old woman under ACOG Practice Bulletin 231 and Committee Opinion 831: chorionicity from the first-trimester scan, aneuploidy screening options in twins (nuchal translucency for each fetus, cell-free DNA, and diagnostic testing), nausea and vomiting of pregnancy, iron deficiency anemia and iron, low-dose aspirin because a twin pregnancy is a high-risk factor for preeclampsia (USPSTF 2021), detailed anatomy scans, growth scans every four weeks with discordance, preterm birth risk and cervical length without cerclage, progesterone, or bed rest for twins alone (ACOG Practice Bulletin 234), gestational diabetes screening, Tdap and seasonal vaccines, a viral gastroenteritis with tightenings at 22 weeks, perinatal mood screening with the EPDS (ACOG Clinical Practice Guideline 4), and a delivery plan for 38 0/7 to 38 6/7 weeks. The episode ends at 33 weeks, before delivery.
