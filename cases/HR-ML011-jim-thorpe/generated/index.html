Social history

  • S Former Olympic champion and professional football and baseball player. Since then has worked in construction, security work and film work, served in the merchant marine, and now speaks at schools and community events and coaches young athletes
  • S Sun exposure: decades of outdoor training, play and construction work, rarely with a hat or lip protection; two blistering sunburns of the lips that he remembers
  • S Tobacco: smoked about a pack of cigarettes a day from his twenties until his heart attack eight years ago (about 35 pack-years); no smokeless tobacco since
  • S Alcohol: drank heavily in earlier years and cut down on his own after his heart attack; now one or two beers on some weekends (AUDIT-C 2)
  • S Enrolled member of the Sac and Fox Nation; lives with his wife in a small house he rents; has health insurance through a public program.
