Biography.com says it was later revealed that she was diagnosed around the time she left Congress (CLM-0006, graded probable); a search summary attributed a 1973 diagnosis to an encyclopedia entry, but the entry fetched did not contain it, so 1973 is not used. (3) Voice of America gives January 18, 1996, as her death date; the obituary, the House history, the Texas State Historical Association and the State Cemetery give January 17, which is preferred. (4) The cane and her “bum knee” explanation rest on one posthumous article (CLM-0005, graded probable).
