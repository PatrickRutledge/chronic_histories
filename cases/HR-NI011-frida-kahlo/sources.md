## Editorial resolutions

- **Roster correction.** The roster files Kahlo under “19th Century,” which does not fit a birth in 1907. The case uses “Early 20th Century.” Nationality is “Mexican.”
- **Index age.** The modern episode presents Kahlo at 37, her age in 1944, when limited mobility made her stop attending her classes at La Esmeralda. The index visit is September 17, 2026, the anniversary of the 1925 accident; the true-date frame places it on September 17, 1944. Past medical history and family history include only events before then. The 1950 hospitalization, the 1953 amputation, and her death remain in the historical timeline.
- **Modern diagnosis.** The documented injuries and chronic pain are **D**. The synthetic episode codes chronic pain due to trauma (G89.21), post-traumatic ankle osteoarthritis (M19.171), and long-term opioid therapy (Z79.891). The opioid history, doses, and taper are **S** and are not a statement about her historical treatment.
- **Contested cause of the leg weakness.** The contemporaneous diagnosis was polio, so the problem list records sequelae of poliomyelitis (B91) with verification provisional. The spina bifida proposal is **R** and stays off the active problem list.
- **Mood.** PHQ-9 scores stay below 10 with item 9 negative, so no depression diagnosis is coded and no structured safety assessment is required. The plan states that one will be done if the score reaches 10 or item 9 is positive.
- **Naloxone.** Naloxone 4 mg nasal spray was dispensed at the first visit, as the care plan records. The case schema now has a nasal route, so the spray is also on the medication list (MED-009, RxNorm 1725059, started at the first visit). Its structured frequency of 0.05 hours encodes the 2-to-3-minute repeat interval on the label, because the schema has no minute unit.
- **Added visits.** Two full encounter notes were added inside the fortnightly taper schedule. An unscheduled sick visit (ENC-005) falls on October 8, 2026, one week after the rehabilitation consultation and one week before the first dose reduction; the true-date frame places it on October 8, 1944. An adult preventive visit (ENC-006) falls on October 22, 2026, one week after the first dose reduction and one week before the six-week review; the true-date frame places it on October 22, 1944. Both are **S**, keep the index age of 37, and precede the 1950 hospitalization, so no documented event is contradicted. The preventive visit follows the influenza dose given at the four-week visit, so the vaccine is recorded as up to date rather than repeated.
- **Sick-visit illness.** A common cold (J06.9), resolved, was chosen because it interacts directly with her regimen without echoing any documented illness: she is already at the 3 g acetaminophen limit; a typical nighttime cold product combines acetaminophen, dextromethorphan (serotonergic with duloxetine 60 mg), and doxylamine (sedating with oxycodone); codeine cough syrups, and oral NSAIDs on top of duloxetine, are avoided; overlapping symptoms are distinguished from opioid withdrawal while the dose is unchanged; and ritonavir-boosted nirmatrelvir would raise oxycodone levels if she tested positive for COVID-19. The note allows the first reduction to move by a week if she were still unwell, as the taper plan permits; it went ahead on schedule.
- **Preventive-visit sensitivities.** Her miscarriages, the pelvic wound, and her marriage history stay unrecorded. Pregnancy intention and contraception were asked privately; she preferred not to discuss her reproductive history, and the note records only that the choice was respected. No statement or decision is attributed to her spouse, and the health care agent is not named. A self-collected HPV sample was chosen because lithotomy positioning aggravates her documented back and pelvic injuries; abdominal and pelvic scars are not described. The intimate partner violence screen is recorded as a routine negative screen with no further detail. AUDIT-C (1), no current tobacco use, and no nonprescribed drug use are modern-frame synthetic findings, not statements about her historical habits. PHQ-9 (5) and GAD-7 (4) stay below the thresholds that would require a structured safety assessment, between the PHQ-9 scores of 6 and 4 on either side.
