Social, functional, and mental status

Social history

  • S Occupation: painter who works from home and teaches art; has had to stop teaching at the art school because of limited mobility, and some students now come to her studio
  • S Married; lives in the family home she grew up in
  • S No sedative, benzodiazepine, or sleep-medication prescriptions; the prescription drug monitoring program shows a single prescriber
  • S Supports: spouse, a younger sister nearby, former students, and a wide circle of friends in the arts

Functional status

  • S Walks short distances with a cane and a limp from the shorter right leg; uses a wheelchair for longer outings; wears a back brace on bad days
  • S Paints two to three hours a day, often seated or reclining; pain limits standing at the easel

Mental status

  • S Alert, warm, witty, and direct; describes pain vividly and with humor
  • S Mood frustrated and low at times because of pain and lost mobility; PHQ-9 in the mild range with no thoughts of self-harm
