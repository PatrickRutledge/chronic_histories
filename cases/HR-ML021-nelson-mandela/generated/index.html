Teaching objective: Localized prostate cancer found by a rising PSA in a fit man of 82, on the NCCN prostate guideline, the AUA/ASTRO 2022 localized prostate cancer guideline, and the CMID prostate cancer monograph: risk grouping and staging (unfavorable intermediate risk, with PSMA PET); shared decision-making among observation, radiotherapy with short-term androgen deprivation, and surgery, with life expectancy and an earlier prostate operation weighed; six months of androgen deprivation with radiotherapy, and the monitoring it needs (testosterone, bone density and vitamin D, glucose and lipids, blood pressure, hot flashes, mood, and cognition); urinary and bowel effects of radiotherapy; and PSA surveillance after radiotherapy with the Phoenix definition of recurrence. His doctors' public account, a slight PSA rise on monitoring, a biopsy, hormone therapy, and a seven-week course of curative radiotherapy, frames the episode.
