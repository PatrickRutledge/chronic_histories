Judgment calls (dossier editorial resolutions)

  1. Index age and dates. Index age 25. The brief suggested an index date in December 1950 or early 1951. Visits in 2026 can only map to January through October of a true year; with a shift of 75 the episode would fall from January 1951 onward, when the sources place her in hospital in Milledgeville and Atlanta and then on ACTH, and the synthetic outpatient visits would contradict that. A shift of 76 instead maps the index visit on April 13, 2026 to April 13, 1950, when she was 25, in the year the sources say her joint pain began and rheumatoid arthritis was suspected, and before the December 1950 illness in which lupus was diagnosed. The other visits (April 22 and 29, June 15, July 27, October 5, and October 26, 2026) map to the same days of 1950, all before that December. The index age of 25 matches the brief.
  2. Diagnosis date. Sources date her diagnosis to 1950 (PBS, New Georgia Encyclopedia, Hektoen), late 1950 in Atlanta (Pharos, Scribner), or January 1951 (a search summary of a Georgia Historical Society page, not used). The timeline records it as "1950, by December," approximate. A disability-studies article seen in search said a specialist told her in July 1952; it was not fetched and is not used.
  3. The 1964 operation. Sources describe it as surgery for a fibroid tumor (PBS, New Georgia Encyclopedia), removal of a benign abdominal tumor (Scribner), or a hysterectomy (Pharos).   4. Avascular necrosis. Crutches, hip pain, and her letter that her doctors blamed the steroid drugs for her "bone disintegration" are documented (D). The responses (a ratio falling from 1.8 to 0.9 g/g at three months and 0.35 at six months) are synthetic and on the favorable side.
