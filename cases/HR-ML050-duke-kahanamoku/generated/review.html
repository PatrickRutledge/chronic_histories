  1. Sources.   3. Heart attack date (correction to the brief). The brief cited a heart attack in 1955. No source read gives 1955; the Star-Advertiser gives 1952, and EBSCO says he had several heart attacks without dates. The record uses 1952 (CLM-0009, certainty “probable”).

The Star-Advertiser records a cerebral blood clot in 1962 and EBSCO a brain operation; no source read mentions a tumor. It is recorded as later history (CLM-0010) and not used in the modern episode.

The index visit on March 16, 2026 maps to March 16, 1957, when he was 66 and sheriff of Honolulu, five years after the 1952 heart attack and five years before the 1962 operation.
