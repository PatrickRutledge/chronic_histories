History of Present Illness

Mr. Bridger came in the same day after falling on his porch steps early this morning, three weeks after cataract surgery on his right eye. He says the right eye now sees sharply and brightly while the left is still blurred, and the two do not agree: the edge of the steps looked closer than it was, he missed the second step and put out his right hand to catch himself. He did not hit his head or his face, did not lose consciousness and got up by himself. His right wrist has been aching and swollen since, worse with gripping. He has no numbness in the hand. His right eye has no pain, redness or new blurring since the fall, and he is still putting in the steroid drops twice a day as the taper allows. He takes no blood thinners. He has used the diclofenac gel on his knees and put ice on the wrist. [S]
