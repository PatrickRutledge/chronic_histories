Teaching objective: Low vision in an older man who works outdoors: a 70-year-old retired backcountry guide, who cannot read and takes in instructions by ear, comes in with two years of painless blurring in both eyes, glare and a fall. The episode covers the history and examination of gradual vision loss and its differential (cataract, glaucoma, age-related macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy), with HbA1c, a dilated examination, intraocular pressure and macular OCT; the decision for surgery from functional impairment rather than an acuity cutoff; optical biometry, sequential phacoemulsification with lens implants in the worse eye first and the second eye six weeks later (with immediately sequential bilateral surgery discussed), postoperative drops and visits under the 1869 American Academy of Ophthalmology Cataract in the Adult Eye Preferred Practice Pattern; falls from low vision and from the mismatch between the eyes between operations; driving and the vision standard; chronic low back pain at the site of an old arrow wound, with an X-ray to look for a retained metal fragment (and before any MRI), and exercise therapy without opioids under the 1865 American College of Physicians guideline; a long-standing multinodular goiter with thyroid function and ultrasound under the 1863 American Thyroid Association guideline; knee osteoarthritis with exercise and topical diclofenac; and spoken, teach-back instructions for a patient who does not read.
