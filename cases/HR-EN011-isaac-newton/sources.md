## Editorial resolutions

- **Calendar and dates.** England used the Julian (Old Style) calendar throughout his life, with the legal year beginning on 25 March. He was born on 25 December 1642 (Julian), which is 4 January 1643 in the Gregorian calendar, and died on 20 March 1726/7 (Julian), written 1727 by the Abbey and Brewster, which is 31 March 1727 (Gregorian). The difference was ten days until February 1700 and eleven days after. The record converts every documented date to the Gregorian calendar for the true frame, as other records with Old Style dates do; the conversions were checked by computing Julian day numbers in the build script. The preferred birth and death dates are Gregorian, with the Julian dates recorded as superseded alternatives, and timeline displays give both. Huygens wrote in the Netherlands, and the source does not state which calendar he used; his date of 29 May 1694 is recorded as Brewster gives it.
- **Index age and dates.** The episode presents him at 50, at Cambridge, in the autumn of 1693, just after the documented letters of September 1693 to Pepys and Locke.
