Teaching objective: Primary care for a 30-year-old professional musician with progressive, asymmetric bilateral sensorineural hearing loss and persistent bothersome tinnitus: a targeted history and examination with tuning forks, a comprehensive audiologic evaluation with word recognition, a search for treatable causes and an MRI for the asymmetry, hearing aids and assistive listening fitted for a musician, sound therapy and cognitive behavioral therapy for tinnitus rather than medication, hearing protection, communication access and workplace accommodation, telling cerumen impaction apart from sudden sensorineural loss, and screening for the depression and isolation that hearing loss can bring, with a structured safety assessment and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Deafness is presented as a change in how he communicates and works, not only as a loss, with Deaf community resources offered on his terms. Built on the AAO-HNS tinnitus, age-related hearing loss, sudden hearing loss, and cerumen guidelines and the CMID depression monograph.
