Teaching objective: Evaluate new weakness, numbness, and clawing of the right hand in a 65-year-old left-handed artist after a fall onto the elbow: localize the lesion by examination (ulnar sensory split of the ring finger, dorsal ulnar sensory loss, intrinsic and deep flexor weakness, Froment and Wartenberg signs, Tinel and elbow flexion tests) and keep stroke in the differential, excluded by a normal neurological examination and brain MRI; elbow X-ray for fracture; nerve conduction studies and EMG to AANEM criteria and nerve ultrasound to confirm ulnar neuropathy at the elbow and exclude C8 radiculopathy and plexopathy; conservative care (night splint, elbow pad, avoiding pressure and prolonged flexion, hand therapy with an anti-claw splint and adapted tools) and a shared decision on surgery (in situ decompression, transposition, or later tendon transfers) following AAOS guidance and the Cochrane review; keeping him working with his dominant left hand; and a stroke risk-factor screen (blood pressure, lipids, HbA1c, and an ECG for atrial fibrillation) with a shared decision on a statin.

Procedures
