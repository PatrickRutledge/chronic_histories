Teaching objective: Primary care and travel medicine for a 43-year-old naval commander with recurrent fevers after service in malaria-endemic areas, who is preparing to lead a deployment to French Guiana and the French Antilles: recognizing relapsing Plasmodium vivax malaria after blood-stage treatment without antirelapse therapy, using PCR when smears are negative between attacks, testing G6PD quantitatively before primaquine, radical cure with chloroquine and primaquine, and relapse surveillance; then a full pre-travel consultation under the CDC Yellow Book 2026 and the IDSA travel medicine guideline: one dose of yellow fever vaccine and a valid certificate for French Guiana, hepatitis A, hepatitis B and typhoid vaccines, atovaquone-proguanil chemoprophylaxis, mosquito avoidance for dengue and chikungunya (no dengue vaccine for travelers), repellents for typhus, food and water precautions with standby treatment for travelers' diarrhea, terminal antirelapse therapy and fever evaluation after return; preventive care at 43; and a same-day visit for a skin abscess managed by drainage alone under the IDSA skin and soft tissue guideline.
