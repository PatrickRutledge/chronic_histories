Teaching objective: A neurocognitive disorder after repetitive head impacts and a later assault, with chronic PTSD, in a 67-year-old retired world champion boxer who comes with his care partner because of memory loss and confusion. The episode follows the 2024 Alzheimer's Association DETeCD-ADRD guideline (history from him and an informant, the MoCA and CDR, function, reversible causes, a medicine review, brain MRI and neuropsychological testing, disclosure and counseling) and shows how the NINDS 2021 criteria for traumatic encephalopathy syndrome are research criteria, not a clinical diagnosis; how PTSD, depression and moral injury (guilt and self-blame after a fight in which his opponent died) are screened and treated under the VA/DoD 2023 PTSD guideline, with cognitive processing therapy adapted for memory problems, sertraline with sodium checks, and a structured safety assessment with the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline; why no cholinesterase inhibitor is used for mild cognitive impairment; falls; caregiver support; and advance care planning while he can take part fully. It respects the way he described his own life and sexuality, and makes no claim about his private health beyond the public record.
