## Advance care planning

Voluntary advance care planning discussion of about 30 minutes with him and his care partner, at his request, while he can take part fully. His capacity for these decisions was assessed: he understood what a health care agent does, explained in his own words why he chose his agent, and gave consistent answers. He signed a New York health care proxy naming his care partner as his health care agent, with a family member on the islands as the alternate; neither is named here. He said that what matters most is staying in his own home, being with the people he loves, being treated with respect, and not being a burden. If his memory one day fails to the point that he no longer knows the people close to him, he wants care focused on comfort, at home if possible, with no feeding tube and no machines to keep him alive; he wants his care partner and family to be able to visit and stay with him wherever he is. Medical Orders for Life-Sustaining Treatment were explained for a later stage and are not needed now. A financial power of attorney was discussed and he will arrange it. The plan will be revisited every year and at any change in his health. **S**
