Teaching objective: Diagnose acral melanoma promptly in a 32-year-old Black man whose dark toenail band was first put down to a football injury: recognize the warning signs of nail melanoma (a band wider than 3 mm, irregular in color and spacing, that does not grow out, with pigment on the nail fold), use dermoscopy, and obtain a full-thickness nail matrix biopsy within the week; stage by AJCC 8th edition and offer sentinel lymph node biopsy for a T1b melanoma; plan surgery with a surgical oncologist through a documented shared decision that sets out amputation and digit-sparing excision of the whole nail unit, chooses digit-sparing surgery where it is oncologically appropriate, and respects his values and his faith; decide on adjuvant therapy by stage; set up surveillance; and teach skin self-examination of the palms, soles, between the toes and all the nails, the sites people with darker skin most need to check, on the NCCN cutaneous melanoma guideline, the AAD primary melanoma guideline, and the ASCO-SSO sentinel node guideline.

Procedures and immunizations

Procedures

  • S Dermoscopy of the right great toenail by the clinic's dermatologist: a brown-black background band about 5 mm wide with longitudinal lines irregular in color, thickness and spacing, and pigment on the proximal nail fold and hyponychium (micro-Hutchinson sign); suspicious for melanoma
  • S Full-thickness excisional biopsy of the nail matrix at the origin of the band, after reflecting the proximal nail fold, under a digital block: acral lentiginous melanoma, Breslow thickness 0.8 mm, not ulcerated, mitotic rate 1 per mm2, no lymphovascular or perineural invasion, melanoma in situ extending to the lateral edges of the specimen
  • S Advance care planning conversation, about 16 minutes
  • S Preoperative lymphoscintigraphy with SPECT/CT after injection around the right great toe: drainage to two right superficial inguinal sentinel nodes; no popliteal drainage
  • S Digit-sparing en bloc excision of the whole nail unit of the right great toe (nail plate, matrix, bed, hyponychium and nail folds) down to the bone, including the periosteum of the distal phalanx, with 5 to 10 mm margins. Pathology: residual melanoma in situ over 3 mm at the biopsy site and no residual invasive melanoma; all margins clear, the closest 6 mm
  • S Full-thickness skin graft from the inner right upper arm to the defect on the right great toe, with a tie-over dressing
  • S Right inguinal sentinel lymph node biopsy: two sentinel nodes removed, both negative for melanoma on serial sections and immunohistochemistry (SOX10 and Melan-A). Final stage pT1b pN0(sn) M0, AJCC 8th edition pathologic stage IA
