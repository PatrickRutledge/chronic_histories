Teaching objective: Provide primary care survivorship care for a premenopausal woman of 45 after mastectomy for stage IA hormone receptor-positive breast cancer: monitor adjuvant tamoxifen (vasomotor symptoms, abnormal bleeding, and clot symptoms), surveil the remaining breast with a yearly mammogram and no routine blood tests or body imaging, counsel on lymphedema risk and bone health, screen for distress, arrange the germline genetic assessment missed at diagnosis, and respect her informed decision not to have reconstruction or wear a prosthesis.
