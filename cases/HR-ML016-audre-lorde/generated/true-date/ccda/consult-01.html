Assessment

  • Stage IA (pT1c pN0) hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative right breast cancer after mastectomy (C50.411). Low risk of recurrence: small, node-negative, grade 2, with a recurrence score of 14, so chemotherapy is not indicated. Radiation is not needed after mastectomy with clear margins and negative nodes. For a premenopausal woman at this risk, tamoxifen alone is standard; ovarian function suppression adds benefit mainly at higher risk and is not recommended for her. An aromatase inhibitor would need ovarian suppression while she is premenopausal. [S]
  • Tamoxifen side effects (Z79.810). Mild hot flashes, common in the first year and often easing. Medicines that strongly inhibit CYP2D6 (paroxetine, fluoxetine, bupropion) may reduce the activation of tamoxifen and should be avoided. [S]
  • Surveillance. For stage I to III breast cancer without symptoms, follow-up is history, examination, and a yearly mammogram of the remaining breast. Routine blood counts, chemistry, tumor markers, and body imaging do not improve survival and are not recommended. Breast MRI is for women who meet high-risk criteria. [S]
  • Germline genetic assessment. Testing is recommended for all patients with breast cancer diagnosed at 65 or younger, and she was 44. A pathogenic variant would change her surveillance (adding breast MRI and possibly gynecologic risk reduction) and would matter to her relatives; a negative result would leave her plan unchanged.
