# Audie Murphy pilot sources

Status: preliminary evidence dossier. The case is not ready for clinical generation or public release.

## Government sources

1. Arlington National Cemetery. “Audie Murphy.” Supports military chronology, wounds, death, postwar symptoms, retrospective PTSD description, and the reported firearm-under-pillow statement. https://arlingtoncemetery.mil/Explore/Notable-Graves/Medal-of-Honor-Recipients/World-War-II-MoH-recipients/Audie-Murphy
2. U.S. Department of Defense. “Medal of Honor Monday: Army Maj. Audie Murphy,” January 27, 2025. Corroborates birth date, service history, insomnia, depression, and retrospective PTSD description. https://www.defense.gov/News/Feature-Stories/Story/Article/4035082/medal-of-honor-monday-army-maj-audie-murphy/
3. U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, Veterans Legacy Memorial. “Audie L Murphy.” Corroborates birth date, death date, rank, service, and decorations. https://www.vlm.cem.va.gov/AUDIELMURPHY/2E228
4. National Archives. “A Heart of Purple.” Context for decorations and archival holdings. https://www.archives.gov/publications/prologue/2012/winter/heart-of-purple

- Any active modern diagnosis, assessment score, vital sign, medication, dose, laboratory result, or treatment response will be `S`.
