## Editorial resolutions

- The roster phrase “eventual sobriety” is not retained as an unqualified fact. The stronger source describes both sustained sober periods and relapse.
- PTSD remains evidence grade **R** because no lifetime contemporary diagnosis has been established.
- Alcohol use disorder and PTSD in the modern episode are grade **S** provisional diagnoses used for interoperability and care-plan testing.
- Captivity abuse is described without graphic detail. No self-harm history is inferred.
- Boyington died in 1988, so the project’s recent-death review rule does not apply; ordinary clinical, provenance, and safe-messaging reviews remain required.
