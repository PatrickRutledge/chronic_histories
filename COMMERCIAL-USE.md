# Commercial use

The Human Record data is free for non-commercial use under [CC BY-NC 4.0](LICENSE-DATA). Commercial use needs a separate license from AetherForge. We grant commercial licenses, and we prefer arrangements that fund more records, clinical review, and tooling.

## When you need a commercial license

You need one if the data, or anything derived from it, is used to earn revenue or to support a commercial product or service. Examples:

- Testing, demonstrating, or certifying an EHR, interface engine, FHIR server, or other commercial health-IT product
- Training, fine-tuning, or evaluating a commercial AI model
- Including the records in a paid course, product, dataset, or benchmark
- Paid consulting or contract work delivered with the records
- Internal use at a for-profit company, including a startup's prototype

## When you do not

- Academic research, teaching, and coursework
- Nonprofit and public-sector projects that are not sold or licensed onward
- Personal study, open-source projects that do not charge for the data, and journalism

If you are unsure, ask. We would rather answer the question than have you guess.

## What we ask for

Terms depend on the use. They can include a license fee, sponsorship of new records or clinical review, contributed engineering or clinician time, or support for the project's work in historical and synthetic health data. Attribution is always required.

## Code

The generators, validators, and schema are under the [MIT License](LICENSE-CODE) and can be used commercially without asking. The commercial license covers the data.

## Contributions

Dual licensing works only if AetherForge holds the rights it licenses. Contributions of data or documentation are accepted only under an agreement that allows them to be included in commercial licenses.
